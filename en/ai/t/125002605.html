Op. 1056B68 SET OF INJECTORS - R + R WITH FUEL MANIFOLD PIPE REMOVED - INCLUDES SEAL REPLACEMENT

bearing in mind that you are working on one injector only.

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the clips (1a) and remove the injectors (1b) from the single fuel manifold pipe.

2. Remove the seals from the injector

Removing

Fit new seals to the injector

Refit the injector into its seat on the single fuel manifold pipe and secure with a clip.
