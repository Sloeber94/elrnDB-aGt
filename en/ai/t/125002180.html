Operational description

The rear fog lamps activation signal reaches pin 35 of connector B of M1

from the control button in the centre console H12

.

The side lights activation signal is sent to pin 28 of connector B of the Body Computer M1

from the steering column switch unit H5

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

.

The dipped headlights activation signal is sent to pin 4 of connector D of the Body Computer M1

from the steering column switch unit H5

EL2011].

The fog lights activation signal reaches pin 1 of connector D of the Body Computer M1

(see EL2030].

The ignition ON signal (INT) for the switch H1

reaches the junction unit under the dashboard B2

, at pin 11 of connector H, and from there reaches the Body Computer M1

(pin 9 of connector F).

The Body Computer M1

controls the power supply for the rear fog light F32

from pin 31 of connector E.

From pin 21 of connector C of M1

a signal is sent for lighting up the LED in the control button H12

.

Via the CAN line the Body Computer M1

is connected to the instrument panel E50

to manage the "lights failure" warning light if a failure is detected in the circuit.
