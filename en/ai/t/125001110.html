Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp

Proceed with removal

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

1. Undo the bolt and remove the bracket joining the exhaust pipes between the preconverters and the catalytic converter.

2. Undo the nuts (2a) and (2b), then remove the left exhaust pipe between the preconverter and the catalytic converter (2c).

Remove the gaskets.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the left exhaust pipe between the preconverter and the catalytic converter (1a), complete with gaskets, back in its housing, then tighten the nuts (1b) and (1c) to the recommended torque.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R.

Remove the car from the ramps.
