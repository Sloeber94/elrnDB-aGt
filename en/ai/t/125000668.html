The seat belt does not lock even when the belt emerges with a jerk

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK LOCKING DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Pull the belt out sharply to check whether the inertia reel locks.

Check the reel is correctly fitted

Continue to Step 2

One front belt does not lock

Replace the front belt Op.

Op. 7065A12 FRONT SEAT BELT (ONE), LH OR RH, WITH PRETENSIONER ON WHEEL - R R

One side rear belt does not lock

Replace the side rear belt Op.

Op. 7065B10 L/H OR R/H REAR SAFETY BELT (ONE) - R+R

The centre rear belt does not lock

Replace the centre rear belt Op.

Op. 7065B18 CENTRE REAR SEAT BELT - R.R.

Incorrect fitting

Continue according to the instructions in

See table

See table O31 SEAT BELTS LOCKED

ROAD TEST

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a road test. Ensure the belt blocks when the car is braked suddenly
