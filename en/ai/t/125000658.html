When the buttons on the front panel of the Connect are pressed the corresponding functions take place, whilst when the buttons on the steering wheel are pressed they do not

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check that there are no errors in the 'reading parameters' ambient at the Connect Node.

Continue to Step 2

Presence of faults

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the Examiner in the 'Reading Parameters' ambient for the Connect Node, check there are no faults and that when the following buttons are pressed and released:

  • Volume + button
  • Volume button
  • Right search button
  • Left search button
  • Input selection button

Continue to Step 4

The button(s) in question does not work (there are no changes in state)

Continue to Step 3

3

ELECTRICAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Continuity of the clock spring wiring
  • Operation of the buttons on the steering wheel SeeSee E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 4

Clock spring wiring faulty

Restore the correct operation of the clock spring wiring.

Buttons the steering wheel faulty

Repair/repalce steering wheel Op.

Op. 4110A11 STEERING WHEEL - R+R FOR REPLACEMENT OR CHECK FOR VERSIONS WITH AIR BAG

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Replace the Connect module Op.

Op. 5580P12 CONNECT PANEL REPLACE
