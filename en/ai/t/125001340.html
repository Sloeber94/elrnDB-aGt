Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the retaining clip (1a), withdraw the bolt (1b), and remove the brake pedal (1c) complete with bushes (1d) and clutch pedal (1e) complete with bushes (1f).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the accelerator pedal with integrated potentiometer (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the accelerator pedal with potentiometer incorporated back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Refit the brake pedal and clutch pedal complete with bushes iin their seats, then secure them with the bolt and retaining clip.
