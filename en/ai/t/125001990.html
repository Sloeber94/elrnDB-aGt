Removing

Check that the anchorage link is not damaged.

If the seat belt has been subjected to great stress, e.g. following an accident, it must be replaced as a complete unit together with its anchorages and the retaining bolts. Even if the seat belt does not show signs of visible defects, it may have lost its resistant properties.

Fit the anchorage link.

Tighten the bolt to the specified torque.
