L10 CLIMATE CONTROL NOT COOLING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fan works properly and sends air into the interior compartment in accordance with the set flows, but this air remains at the external temperature and is not cooled.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The enablement to switch on the refrigerating circuit is controlled by the climate control and engine management control units. So if the air conditioning does not cool under particular conditions, this is correct.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
