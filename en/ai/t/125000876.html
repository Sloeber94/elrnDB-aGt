Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R.

Position a hydraulic jack beneath the gearbox.

1. Undo the nut (1a) and disconnect the earth lead (1b) from the power unit gearbox side rigid support.

Description

Connector

1b

Battery earth

See C1 BATTERY EARTH

2. Unscrew the bolt securing the power unit rigid mount to the flexible block.

3. Undo the bolts (3a) and remove the power unit gearbox side rigid support (3b).

One of the above bolts also secures the power unit mounting bracket.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods
