Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: Xenon Gas headlamps

Disconnect the 10-way connector from the headlamp, switch on the dipped headlamps and check that there is battery voltage between pin 1 (+) and pin 5 (-) and that the polarity is correct.

Check the continuity of the wiring between pins 9 and 10 of the headlamp alignment control unit and pins 1 and 5 respectively for the front and rear geometry sensors.

Check the continuity between pins 11 and 12 of the control unit and pin 4 of the front and rear geometry sensors.
