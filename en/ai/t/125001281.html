Refitting

  • Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery terminal (-).

  • Remove the gear selection electro-hydraulic system hydraulic circuit fluid reservoir cap.

  • Loosen the band and disconnect the pipe from the hydraulic circuit fluid reservoir to the gearbox electro-hydraulic selection pump, reservoir side.

  • Loosen the band and disconnect the cold air intake pipe from the air filter.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

  • Working in the space created by removing the light cluster, undo the connector (1a) and disconnect the pipe from the hydraulic circuit fluid reservoir to the gearbox electro-hydraulic selection pump (1b), pump side and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing
  • Place the pipe from the hydraulic circuit fluid reservoir to the gearbox electro-hydraulic selection pump (1a) back in its housing, connect it to the pump and tighten the connector (1b) to the recommended torque.
