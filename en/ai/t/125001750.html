5580T42 BOSE SOUND SYSTEM AMPLIFIER R R

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting
  • Open the tailgate.
  • Disconnect the negative battery terminal.
  • Release the retaining buttons (1a) using the special tool (1b) (1.823.015.000).
  • Remove the amplifier housing trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

  • Op. 7040H80 PARCEL RACK
  • Op. 7065C86 BOOT/LOAD PLATFORM POWER UNIT HOUSING - R R
  • Op. 7040B35 One luggage compartment COMPARTMENT left or right RR
  • Disconnect the electrical connection.
  • Undo the bolts.
  • Remove the amplifier.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing
  • Check that the amplifier is not damaged.
  • Position the amplifier and secure it using the bolts.
  • Connect the electrical connection.
  • Op. 7040B35 One luggage compartment COMPARTMENT left or right RR
  • Op. 7065C86 BOOT/LOAD PLATFORM POWER UNIT HOUSING - R R
  • Op. 7040H80 PARCEL RACK
  • Position the protection and secure it using the retaining buttons.
  • Connect the negative battery terminal.
  • Close the bonnet.
  • Check that the radio system is working properly.
