Position the vehicle on the ramp

Proceed with removal

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and the nuts (1b) and remove the heat shield for the left catalytic preconverter (1c).

Refitting

Removing

Place the heat shield for the left catalytic preconverter back in its housing, then fasten it using the bolt and the nuts.

Proceed with refitting

Op. 1080B92 LEFT UPPER LAMBDA SENSOR R R.

Proceed with refitting

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R.
