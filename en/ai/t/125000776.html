2127M CLUTCH PUSH ROD ADJUSTMENT CHECK

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Turn the ignition key to the ON position.

Connect the Examiner Plus or Smart diagnostic equipment.

Check that the clutch position is between 28 and 28.5 mm.

To adjust the clutch correctly, the locknut must be loosened and then the push rod adjusted until the reading on the Examiner Plus or Smart indicates a clutch position between 28 and 28.5 mm.

The locknut should be tightened to a torque of 0.6 ± 0.05 daNm.
