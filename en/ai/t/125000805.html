5580CB PASSENGER SIDE BAG AND HEAD BAG CHECK

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Disconnect the Bag connector at the line involved and connect the special line closure resistor.

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Cancel the errors and check that they have disappeared.

If the error persists, search for the fault in the wiring or in the control unit.

If the error does not reoccur, the fault should be sought in the Bag.

Reconnect the connector.
