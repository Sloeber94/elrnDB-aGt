Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Functional description

All the electrical equipment and systems are supplied by the battery

A1

.

The main supply lines are protected by the MAXIFUSE box B5 which contains the fuses named according to the circuits protected:

  • ABS: [EL7020]
  • IGN: ignition switch supply H1, pin 2 of connector A
  • A/C: [EL6020]
  • IE: engine management fuses and relays supply (relay T9 of the engine compartment relay junction unit B1 connector B);
  • FAN1: [EL5020];
  • FAN2: [EL5020];
  • JB/3: supply for services via engine compartment junction unit B1, connector C;
  • CPL1: underdashboard junction unit supply B2, at pin A of connector C;
  • CPL2: underdashboard junction unit supply B2, at pin B of connector C

Other circuits are supplied by turning ignition key H1 to various different positions:

  • in the ON position, numerous circuits and services controlled by the ignition (INT line) are supplied ('INT' and '15/54' lines): pin 2 of connector B and 1 of connector A;
  • in the AVV position - the starter motor is supplied; (line '50') [EL5010]; in this position several circuits are disconnected ('INT/A' line): 1 of connector B;
  • . in the PARK position the side lights are supplied ('POS' line); 3 of connector B;
