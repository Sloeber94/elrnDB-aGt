The wind noise persists with the entire bonnet taped

Check for the presence of the plugs and that the cable duct rubber mountings are correctly fitted on the fireproof bulkhead Op. 1072C45

The noise is coming from the side of the bonnet

Check the foam blocks at the point where the bonnet and wings fit together at the rear. Also check the condition of the wind seals and make sure they are properly fitted, if necessary replace them Op.

Op. 7025A62 BONNET LID ANTI-WIND NOISE SEAL - R+R

The noise is coming from the front of the bonnet

Check the condition of the front sound-proofing seal and that it is correctly fitted (it must adhere perfectly to the cross panel when the bonnet is closed), if necessary, replace it. Op.
