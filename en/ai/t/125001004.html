Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Open the fuel filler cap access flap.

2. Fit the fuel filler pipe cap.

3. Remove the cap (3a) releasing it from the device that prevents it from being lost (3b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fasten the cap to the device that prevents it from getting lost.

Tighten the cap in a clockwise direction until one or more clicks are heard.

Close the fuel filler cap access flap.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods
