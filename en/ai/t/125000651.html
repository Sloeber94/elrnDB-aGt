The date and/or the time are incorrect in the Connect system

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

SATELLITE G.P.S. RECEPTION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the satellite reception is good, that the reception area is not underneath trees, high buildings, multi-storey car parks, tunnels or places where satellite reception may be obstructed

Continue to Step 2

Reception poor as signal cannot be easily received

Move to an area that is open with nothing obstructing the reception of the G.P.S. signal

2

SETTINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

In the system configuration, check that the country setting is the correct one and that the representation mode is the one desired

Continue to Step 2

Country setting not correct

Restore the correct setting

The 'SDR time' function which allows hourly information from G.P.S. satellites is not activated

Activate the 'SDR time' function

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
