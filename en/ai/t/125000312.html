CONSTRUCTION FEATURES

Electric fans

The two speed electric cooling fan makes it possible to increase the dissipation of the heat from the radiator and/or the air conditioning system condenser.

It is controlled directly by the injection control unit in accordance with a specific operating logic

Combined sender unit for coolant temperature gauge and overheating warning light

Mounted on the cylinder head, gearbox side, it indicates the coolant temperature and controls the warning light:

  • contacts open 112 ± 3° C
  • contacts closed 122 ± 2° C
