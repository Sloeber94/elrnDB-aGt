The lights do not work when the switches in the vanity mirrors are activated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

LIGHT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • The condition of the bulbs
  • The condition of the bulb holders

Continue to Step 2

Bulbs or courtesy lights faulty

Restore or replace the courtesy light Op.

Op. 7040E21 COURTESY LIGHT FOR SUN VISOR MIRROR (1), LH OR RH - R R

2

MIRROR LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Body Computer power supply;
  • Connections between courtesy light and Body Computer;
  • Courtesy light earth connections SeeSee E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore the correct connections See

See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

Body Computer earth faulty

Restore the earth connection See

See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

Faulty Body Computer supply

Restore connection See

See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

3

CHECK ON SIGNAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that when the lights in the mirrors come on, an earth signal is sent from the Body Computer to the courtesy lights

Continue to Step 4

No earth signal from Body Computer

Restore the correct supply to the Body Computer. If necessary, replace the Body Computer Op.

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R R

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Check that the fault is no longer present
