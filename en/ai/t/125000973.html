Op. 1032B10 TOOTHED TIMING BELT - R + R FOR TIMING ADJUSTMENT OR REPLACEMENT.

PROCEED AS FOLLOWS FOR THE UPPER FIXED TIMING TENSIONER.

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and remove the upper fixed timing tensioner (1b).

PROCEED AS FOLLOWS FOR THE LOWER FIXED TIMING TENSIONER.

2. Undo the bolt (2a) and remove the lower fixed timing tensioner (2b).

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods
