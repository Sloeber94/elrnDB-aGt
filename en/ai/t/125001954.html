Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the protection

3. Remove the steel points.

4. Prise up trim (4a) from padding (4b).

5. Recover the unwoven cloth protection.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the cover is intact and shows no signs of damage.

Position the unwoven cloth protection on the padding.

Fit the padding trim and mould in place.

Apply the steel points.

Position the protection.

Tighten the bolts.
