Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the car in motion, a smell of exhaust gas is noted coming from the rear area.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK AIR OUTLET FITTINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rear air outlet fittings

In particular, ascertain:

  • Fin position (they should open outward)
  • Correct fitting

Continue to Step 2

Incorrect fitting

Restore the fittings to the correct position Op.

Op. 5010C60 LH OR RH REAR AIR OUTLET -ONE- R R

2

CHECKING BOOT LID SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check boot lid seal In particular, check the sealing strip for wrinkles

Continue to Step 3

Faulty fitting of seal

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7025B66

3

BOOT LID ALIGNMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check tail-gate is correctly fitted. In particular, check the boot lid alignment. Ensure the sealing strip is in contact around the entire perimeter.

Continue to Step 4

Boot installation faulty

Refit the boot lid correctly Op. 7025B10

4

CHECK BOOT FRAME SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that boot is sealed correctly.

Continue to Step 5

Boot seal faulty

Restore correct boot seal

5

UNDERBODY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the underbody plugs in the boot area are present

Continue to Step 6

Underbody plugs missing

Refit the missing plugs

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
