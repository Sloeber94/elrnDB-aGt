2127F CHECK ON BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Turn the ignition key to the ON position. Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and select the display of the input state (ON/OFF) for the switch on the brake pedal.

Press the brake pedal and check that the value displayed changes (from released to pressed or viceversa).
