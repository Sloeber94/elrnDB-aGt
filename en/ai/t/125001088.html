Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK; all production periods

Removing

Refitting
  • Disconnect the vacuum intake pipe from the air flow switching device actuator and release it from the fastening on the latter.
  • Disconnect the air flow switching device actuator linkage.
  • Undo the bolts (3a) and remove the air flow switching device control actuator (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK; all production periods

Refitting

Removing
  • Place the air flow switching device control actuator in position.
  • Connect the air flow switching device actuator linkage.
  • Connect the vacuum intake pipe from the air flow switching device actuator and secure it to the latter.
