Position the vehicle on the ramp

Drain the power steering fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

Proceed with removal

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

1. Undo the nut (1a) and remove the front bracket (1b) supporting the power steering pipes.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove rear bracket (1b) supporting the power steering pipes.

2. Disconnect the power steering pump inlet pipe from the fluid reservoir pipe, fluid reservoir pipe side.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Undo the connector (1a) and disconnect the power steering pump inlet pipe from the fluid reservoir pipe (1b), power steering pump side.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Remove the power steering pump inlet pipe from the fluid reservoir pipe, releasing from its rubber mounts.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Connect the power steering pump inlet pipe from the fluid reservoir pipe, fluid reservoir pipe side and tighten the band.

Place the power assisted steering pipes rear mounting bracket back in its housing and secure with the bolt.

Refit the front bracket supporting the engine oil/power steering pipes back in its seating and secure it with the nut.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R.

Fill the power steering circuit with the recommended fluid.

Bleed the power steering system as described below

With the front wheels raised off the ground, with the engine switched off turn the steering wheel to full lock, left and right, 4-5 times.

Check and, if necessary, top up the fluid level in the reservoir.

Remove the car from the ramps.

Start the engine and turn the steering wheel to full lock, left and right, 4 - 5 times.

Top up the fluid level in the reservoir.
