Removing

Refitting

Op. 7040L20 TUNNEL TRIM - R R

1. Working at the bench, inside the tunnel cover, undo the bolts fixing the centre air duct to the rear seats.

2. Remove the air duct (2a), releasing it from the vent (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the centre air duct to the rear seats is not damaged.

Position the duct, fitting it to the vent.

Tighten the bolts.

Op. 7040L20 TUNNEL TRIM - R R
