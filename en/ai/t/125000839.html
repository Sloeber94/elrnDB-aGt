Check the oil level in the manual gearbox with differential with the vehicle level and the engine off and cold.

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Proceed with the removal

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R.

1. Remove the gear casing filler cap and check the oil level reaches the lower edge of the filler hole.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

If necessary, top up the level with the recommended fluid through the filler hole.
