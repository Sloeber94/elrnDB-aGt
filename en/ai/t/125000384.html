INTRODUCTION

For correct driveability of the car it is essential to maintain the design geometry (dimensions and characteristic angles of the front and rear wheels) in the various driving conditions (acceleration, braking, bends).

The position of the wheels is determined by the position of the hubs, which can be adjusted to obtain the values specified for maintenance interventions, to be carried out by authorized Alfa Romeo workshops.
