When the lever located on the right of the steering wheel is activated with the side lights on, the headlamp washer does not work properly

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operation

With the side lights on, check that the steering column switch unit works properly for the windscreen washer function

HEADLAMP WASHER RELAY CHECK (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Swap the headlamp washer relay (in the engine compartment junction unit) with the horn relay (the third one in the same row) and check that the headlamp washers are working properly afterwards

Continue to Step 2

The headlamp washers still do not work even after the swap

Continue to Step 3

REPLACING HORN RELAY (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Replace the horn relay (which was actually the one for the headlamp washer) and check that the horn is working properly

End of diagnosis

.

HEADLAMP WASHER RELAY POWER SUPPLY CHECK (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the headlamp washer relay is correctly supplied by the battery

With the ignition ON, connect to the supply for the relay from the Body Computer and check that the supply is sent to the relay when the headlamp washer is activated.

Also check that the electrical connections between the Body Computer, the battery and the relay are not faulty See

See E3022 HEADLAMP WASHER

Do not connect the relay

Continue to Step 4

No supply from battery

Restore the correct supply to the relay. If necessary replace the faulty components (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Supply enablement not provided by the Body Computer

Continue to Step 5

Connections faulty
