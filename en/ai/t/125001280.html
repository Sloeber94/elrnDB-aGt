Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting
  • Position the vehicle on a lift.
  • Remove the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox hydraulic circuit fluid reservoir cap.
  • Drain the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox hydraulic circuit fluid reservoir.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

  • Undo the bolts and remove the control unit guard.
  • Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL R R
  • Op. 7055B90 LEFT FRONT WHEEL ARCH LINER - R.R.
  • Loosen the band and disconnect the pipe from the hydraulic circuit fluid reservoir to the electro-hydraulic gear selection pump, reservoir side.
  • Loosen the band and disconnect the pipe from the electro-hydraulic selection activation hydraulic unit to the fluid reservoir, reservoir side.
  • Undo the bolt (3a) and the nut (3b) and remove the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox hydraulic circuit fluid reservoir (3c).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing
  • Place the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox hydraulic circuit fluid reservoir back in its housing and secure it using the bolt and the nut.
  • Connect the pipe from the electro-hydraulic selection activation hydraulic unit to the fluid reservoir, reservoir side and tighten the band.
  • Connect the pipe from the hydraulic circuit fluid reservoir to the electro-hydraulic gear selection pump, reservoir side and tighten the band.
  • Op. 7055B90 LEFT FRONT WHEEL ARCH LINER - R.R.
  • Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL R R
  • Place the control unit guard in its housing and secure it using the bolts.
  • Op. 0010T47 HYDRAULIC SYSTEM FLUID FOR GEARBOX WITH HYDRAULIC SELECTION CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY
  • Remove the vehicle from the lift.
