Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the key is turned to the ON position, the instruments light up; in the (AVV) Starting position, the starter motor does not turn.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

MOTOR POWER SUPPLY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the supply lead between the battery and the starter motor is intact and securely fastened

Move on to Step 2

Lead fastening slack

Secure the lead

2

STARTER MOTOR RELAY FEED CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the connection between the switch and relay

Move on to Step 3

No continuity

Using a Tester, check whether the lack of continuity is between the switch and the engine compartment junction unit or between the engine compartment junction unit and the motor relay: see wiring diagram

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

Junction unit continuity failure

If the lack of continuity is in the junction unit, replace the junction unit

Op. 5505A13 SUPPLEMENTARY JUNCTION CONTROL UNIT IN ENGINE BAY - R R

otherwise, renew the cable concerned

3

SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the operation and the condition of the switch.

Operate the switch.

Move on to Step 4

No contact at pin 50

Replace the switch

Op. 5520A12 IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY - R R FOR VERSIONS WITH AIR BAG

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Search for the problem starting from another symptom
