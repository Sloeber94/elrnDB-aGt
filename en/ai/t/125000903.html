Removing

Place the left cylinder head gasket back in its housing.

Refit the left cylinder head.

1. Tighten the nuts (1a) fixing the left cylinder head to the recommended torque using the angular tightening tools (1b) and (1c).

Lubricate the threads, nuts and washers with engine oil.

Follow the order shown in the diagram for each tightening sequence.
