The interior timed lights work when controlled manually, but the dimmer doesn't work when the doors are opened.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When one of the side doors is opened, the courtesy light is turned on for a period of 3 minutes and the lights are switched on/off gradually (dimmer effect). When the car is unlocked after activation of the remote control, the courtesy light is switched on for a period of 15 seconds, but it is switched off by the ignition ON signal. If the ignition ON signal is present following the opening of a door, the courtesy lights come on.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
