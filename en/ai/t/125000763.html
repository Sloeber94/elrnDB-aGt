Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK; all production periods

Disconnect the negative battery terminal

  • Disconnect the negative battery terminal.
  • Disconnect the electrical connection for the upstream Lambda sensor.
  • Check that the heater resistance measured between pins 3 and 4 is between 3.5 and 4.5 Ohm at 20 °C.
