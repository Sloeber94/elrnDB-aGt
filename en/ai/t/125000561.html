Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the lever on the right of the steering wheel is pulled towards the latter, the windscreen washer function does not work properly

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a preliminary check on the fluid level in the reservoir; also make sure that it is not leaking.

Check that when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is operated, the windscreen wipers work properly.

Check that the jets are not obstructed by ice or dirt

Continue to Step 1

Mixture level too low

Top up the level Op.

Op. 5050A14 RESERVOIR PLUG (IN ENGINE BAY) OR WINDSCREEN WASHER FLUID LEVEL SENSOR - CHECK AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY

Reservoir damaged

Replace the reservoir Op.

Op. 5050A10 WINDSCREEN WASHER FLUID RESERVOIR (IN ENGINE BAY) - R+R

Jets dirty or blocked

Clean the jets Op.

Op. 5050B10 FRONT WINDSCREEN WASHER JETS - R+R CLEAN AND/OR POSITION
