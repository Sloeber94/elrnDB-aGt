Turn the crankshaft until the pistons of the 1st and 4th cylinder are set to BDC.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the connecting rod caps (1b) complete with half-bearings (1c) for cylinders 1 and 4.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Withdraw and remove the piston/connecting rod assemblies (1a) complete with half-bearings (1b) for cylinders 1 and 4.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Work in the same way to remove the piston/connecting rod assemblies for cylinders 2 and 3.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the piston/connecting rod assemblies (1a) for cylinders 2 and 3 complete with half-bearings (1b) in their housings using a suitable fitting tool.

The piston-connecting rod assemblies should be fitted so that the arrows stamped on the piston crown are facing the exhaust side.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Place the connecting rod caps (1a) for cylinders 2 and 3 in their seats complete with half-bearings (1b) and secure them by tightening the bolts (1c) to the recommended torque.

The references on the connecting rod cap and the big end should be on the same side.
