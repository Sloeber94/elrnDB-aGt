Before altering the position of the door striker, ensure the door is properly aligned.

Op. 7005A14 FRONT SIDE DOOR - ADJUST CENTERING AND MOULDING - INCLUDES ADAPTATION OF HINGES AND SEALS ON DOOR COMPARTMENT.

1. Loosen the striker retaining screws.

2. Use the bolt slots to move the striker into the correct position.

Fit shims if necessary in order to adjust the striker accurately. Permitted adjustment movements are indicated by arrows.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Tighten the fixing bolts.
