3340B56 RIGID PIPE FROM HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT TO RIGHT FRONT BRAKE - R.R.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Proceed with the removal

Op. 4450B04 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL R R.

Remove the protective cap from the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

Connect the recovery device (pipe and reservoir) to the brake caliper bleed valve.

Loosen the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

Operate the brake pedal until the brake/clutch reservoir is drained.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the brake-clutch fluid minimum level warning light switch.

2. Undo the connectors (2a) and (2b) and remove the rigid pipes from the master cylinder to the A.B.S. hydraulic control unit (2c).

3. Undo the connector (3a) and disconnect the rigid pipe from the hydraulic control unit to the right front brake (3b), hydraulic control unit side.

Release the rigid pipe from the hydraulic control unit to the right front brake from the fastening on the control unit support.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Place the rigid pipe from the hydraulic control unit to the right front brake in position.

Fasten the fuel supply pipe and the fuel vapour recovery pipe to the underbody fastenings.

Place the protection for the fuel supply pipe and for the fuel vapour recovery pipe in position and secure them using the bolts.

Secure the rigid pipe from the hydraulic control unit to the right front brake to the fastening in the wheel arch.

1. Connect the rigid pipe from the hydraulic control unit to the right front brake (1a), flexible pipe side and tighten the connector (1b) to the recommended torque.
