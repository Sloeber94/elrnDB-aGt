CONSTRUCTION FEATURES

The driveshafts, the intermediate shaft and the constant velocity joints make up the 'axle' assembly and transmit the movement from the gearbox differential to the drive wheels.

The presence of an intermediate shaft is due to the need to have the right and left driveshafts of the same length, with the advantages of maintaining direction during acceleration and less vibration.
