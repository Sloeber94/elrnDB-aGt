Removing

Refitting

Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Connector

-

Battery

See A1B BATTERY ( BATTERY NEGATIVE )

Proceed with the removal

Op. 7040L40 REAR COMPARTMENT SCUFF STRIP - R R.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the inertia switch (1b) after having disconnected the electrical connection (1c).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Connect the electrical connection to the inertia switch.

Place the inertia switch in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Proceed with the refitting

Op. 7040L40 REAR COMPARTMENT SCUFF STRIP - R R.
