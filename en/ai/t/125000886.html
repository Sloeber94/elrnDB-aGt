Proceed with removal

Op. 1008C10 POWER UNIT UPPER REACTION LINK R R.

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and remove the upper bracket on the bodyshell for the reaction rod (1b).

The above mentioned nuts also fix the right front shock absorber to the bodyshell.

2. Undo the nut (2a) and remove the mounting bracket for the wheel rpm sensor connection and fuel pipes (2b).

3. Undo the nut (3a) and the bolt (3b) and remove the lower bracket on the bodyhsell for the reaction rod (3c).

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods
