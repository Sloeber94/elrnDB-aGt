When taking off, there is a noise when the various gears are engaged and the clutch is released or at the start of deceleration

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • Kingpin attachment
  • Shock absorber/suspension fastening
  • Condition and attachment of bushes and flexible mountings securing power unit and moving suspension components
  • Condition and play on steering balljoints

Continue to Step 2

Wheel fastenings slack

Retighten attachments

Power unit flexible mountings damaged

Replace the damaged components

Track control arm flexible mountings damaged

Replace track control arms

2

CHECKING THE GEAR SET

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the crownwheel and pinion are worn or damaged, and that the crownwheel attachments are not damaged

Continue to Step 3

Crownwheel and pinion worn

Replace the crownwheel and pinion

3

CHECK ON DIFFERENTIAL UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the wear of the satellite carrier bearing or the housing in the differential housing

Check differential bearing preload

Continue to Step 4

Housing worn

Replace casing

Preload not correct

Adjust preload as specified

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
