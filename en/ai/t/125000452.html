When the alarm is operated, the direction indicators do not flash

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

The direction indicators may or may not be flashing, according to the various country modes.

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket and check whether there are faults on the CAN network

Continue to Step 2

Faults present in the CAN network

Repair the connections on the nodes concerned

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the diagnostic equipment carry out active diagnosis of the operation of the direction indicators.

Continue to Step 3

Lights not activated

Replace the Body Computer Op.

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R R

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
