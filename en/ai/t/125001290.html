Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting
  • Op. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R
  • Op. 5530B52 BATTERY DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox position sensor.

Description

Connector

1

Gear selector potentiometer

See K68 SPEED SELECTION POTENTIOMETER

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox gear selector position sensor (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing
  • Place the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox gear selector position sensor back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.
  • Connect the electrical connection to the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox position sensor.
  • Op. 5530B52 BATTERY DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R.
  • Op. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R
