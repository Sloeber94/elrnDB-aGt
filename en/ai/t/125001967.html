Refitting

Op. 7015A04 TAILGATE INTERIOR PANEL - R+R.

1. Working from inside the tailgate, undo the badge retaining nut.

2. Extract the badge from the outer part of the tailgate releasing it from the centering pins.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Check that the badge is undamaged.

Fit the crest by engaging the locating pins.

Tighten the badge fixing nut.
