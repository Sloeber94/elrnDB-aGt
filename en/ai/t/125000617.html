Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

On starting, the passenger Air Bag disablement warning light does not come on (it should come on for 4 seconds to indicate the initial test on the Air Bag control unit, and then go out)

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check that there is a regular dialogue with the Air Bag control unit

Continue to Step 2

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between Air Bag control unit and diagnostic connector and Air Bag control unit supply See

See E7030 AIRBAG

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECKING SPEEDOMETER POWER SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Connection between airbag control unit and speedometer;
  • Speedometer power supply SeeSee E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Defective component

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

3

SPEEDOMETER CHECK

Continue to Step 4
