PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Resting the foot on the brake pedal, check that the pedal sinks when the engine is started, because of the vacuum in the braking circuit

End of diagnosis

Absence of or partial servo assistance

Continue to Step 1

CHECK ON VACUUM PIPE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check vacuum pipe: must not be bent or damaged

Continue to Step 2

Pipe bent

Restore the correct geometry

Pipe damaged

Replace the damaged pipe Op. 3330D24

CHECK ON NON-RETURN VALVE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the non-return valve is working properly

Faulty operation

Replace the check valve Op. 3330D20

BRAKE SERVO PNEUMATIC CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check in particular:

  • that there are no air leaks
  • that there are no seizures or stiffness

Continue to Step 4

Brake servo with leaks

Replace the brake servo/pump assembly Op.

Op. 3330D02 BRAKE SERVO/BRAKE PUMP ASSEMBLY - R R

Brake servo operation is jerky

Replace the brake servo/pump assembly Op.

Op. 3330D02 BRAKE SERVO/BRAKE PUMP ASSEMBLY - R R

4

CHECK ON VACUUM

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the vacuum level in the servo brake system

Under particular conditions of use, low engine rpm with excessive use of the brakes, the vacuum for operating the servo brake may be insufficient resulting in hardening of the brake pedal. It is pointless replacing the brakeservo.

Continue to Step 5

Value incorrect

Eliminate blockages in the manifold

Value correct

Replace the brake servo/pump assembly Op.

Op. 3330D02 BRAKE SERVO/BRAKE PUMP ASSEMBLY - R R

5

CHECK ON PUMP

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the brake pump has not seized.

Check that one or more wheels do not remain braked

Continue to Step 6

Seizure of the pump

Replace the brake servo/pump assembly Op.

Op. 3330D02 BRAKE SERVO/BRAKE PUMP ASSEMBLY - R R

Seizure of a front caliper

Replace the caliper Op.

Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER ONE R R

Seizure of a rear caliper

Replace the caliper Op.

Op. 3310B36 REAR BRAKE CALLIPER - R R

The car remains braked

Remove the servo/pump assembly Op.

Op. 3330D02 BRAKE SERVO/BRAKE PUMP ASSEMBLY - R R

and adjust the projection of the pushrod

The car remains braked at the rear

Repair the handbrake

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Test the vehicle before delivery to the customer, checking that the fault is no longer present

The test is essential, as the braking system is a safety feature of the car

End of diagnosis
