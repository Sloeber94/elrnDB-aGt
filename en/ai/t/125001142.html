Refitting

Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Temporarily tighten the bolts fixing the rear suspension crossmember.

Remove the hydraulic jack from beneath the crossmember.

1. Undo the nut (1a) and disconnect the flange (1b) for the pipe from the condenser to the intermediate connector, intermediate connector side.

Seal the connectors that have been disconnected using suitable plugs to prevent dampness and impurities from entering the system.

2. Release the air conditioning system pipes from the fastenings on the side panel.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and disconnect the supply and return pipes from the evaporator (1b), evaporator side.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Release the pipe from the fuel vapour separator to the active charcoal filter from the fastenings on the underbody and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the pipe from the fuel vapour separator to the active charcoal filter in position and secure it to the underbody using the fastenings.

1. Connect the fuel supply and return pipes from the evaporator (1a), evaporator side and fasten them tightening the bolt (1b) to the recommended torque.

Replace the O rings for the connectors. Only use green coloured seals that are resistant to R134a refrigerant fluid.Lubricate the connector threads using anti-freeze. .
