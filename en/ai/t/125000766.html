Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Connect the Examiner Plus or Smart diagnostic equipment.

Display the following in parameter mode:

  • Engine rpm
  • Clutch disc speed

Road test the vehicle, at a constant speed (about 30 km/h), without changing gear.

Observe the two parameters selected whilst driving

Check that there are no significant fluctuations/differences in the clutch speed signal compared with the engine speed.
