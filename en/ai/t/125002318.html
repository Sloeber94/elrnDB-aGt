Functional description

The radio P20

receives a direct power supply (7 of connector A) from the battery via the line for fuse F39 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2

and maxifuse CPL of B99

( of B5

for the 3.2 V6 version).

Another specific power supply reaches pin 2 of connector A, to signal the presence of the Bose HIFI system.

The power supply reaches pin 4 of connector A with the side lights on

See E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING

.

The CAN line arrives at pins 1 and 3 of connector A with which the radio P20

is connected to the Body Computer M1

and to the other system nodes.

Pin 8 of connector A is earthed.

The aerial P25 receives a power supply from pin 5 of connector A

. The aerial is connected to the radio by means of the special coaxial cable.

The cables with signals for the speakers leave from connector B; these signals are sent to the BOSE amplifier P70 which controls the actual speakers.

  • pins 5 and 6 of P20 and pins 31 and 32 of P70 to the left front speakers P35 and P45,
  • pins 3 and 4 of P20 and pins 26 and 27 of P70 to the right front speakers P36 and P46,
  • pins 7 and 8 of P20 and pins 4 and 16 of P70 to the left rear speakers P30 and P47,
  • pins 1 and 2 of P20 and pins 5 and 17 of P70 to the right rear speakers P31 and P48,

The connecting cable leaves connector C of P20 (pins 13-20) for the CD player P75 in accordance with the following connections: - Pin 13 asci bus from CDC to the radio - Pin 14 asci bus from the radio to the CDC - Pin 15 +12 permanent - Pin 16 +12V switched - Pin 17 digital earth - Pin 18 audio earth - Pin 19 left audio CDC - Pin 20 right audio CDC

.

Pins 10 and 11 of connector C are connected to the radio controls on the steering wheel: the connection takes place via the clock spring D47

; the device which controls the control buttons receives an ignition-operated supply (INT) from the line protected by fuse F51 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2

, whilst the reference earth arrives from pin 12 of connector C of P20

.

The amplifier P70

receives a direct supply from the battery - at pin 1 - via the line for fuse F2 of B1 and the specific fuse F61 in the additional fuse box B98

.

Pin 6 is then connected with a special line to the radio P20

pin 6 of connector C which sends the on signal for the actual amplifier.

Pin 2 of P70

is earthed.

The other signals leaving the BOSE amplifier P70

control the "basic" system speakers as described previously. In addition, signals leave pins 3-15 and 11-12 which control the rear subwoofer P49

.

At pins 29 and 30 of P70

the signals from the ambient microphone P02 arrive
