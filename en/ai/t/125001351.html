1. Unscrew the bolt securing the power unit rigid mount to the flexible block.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Lower the power unit as necessary using the hydraulic jack.

Working underneath the vehicle, extract the brake servo/brake pump assembly from its housing.

Tighten the bolt securing the gearbox side power unit rigid support to the flexible mounting.

Remove the hydraulic jack from beneath the gearbox

1. Working in the engine compartment, remove the brake servo/brake pump assembly.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Working in the engine compartment, temporarily position the brake servo/brake pump assembly.

Position a hydraulic jack beneath the gearbox.

Unscrew the bolt securing the power unit rigid mount to the flexible block.

Working underneath the vehicle, place the brake servo/brake pump assembly in position.

1. Tighten the bolt securing the power unit gearbox side rigid support to the flexible mounting to the recommended torque.
