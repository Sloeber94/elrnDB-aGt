Operational description

The Cruise Control function is managed by the engine management control unit M10

when controlled by the special Cruise Control system control H7, located on the left of the steering wheel.

The control H7

receives an ignition-controlled power supply (INT) pin 2 from the line protected by fuse F35 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2

Control H7 is connected to control unit M10: - at pin 10 of connector A, with the ON/OFF signal at pin 42 of connector A, with the RCL signal at pin 43 of connector A, with the SET+ signal at pin 458 of connector A, with the SET- signal

Pin 26 of connector A of M10 receives a "pedal released" signal (N.C. contact) coming from the second contact of the brake lights switch I30, with an ignition-operated supply (INT) from the line protected by fuse F35 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2. Pin 59 of connector A of M10 receives a signal coming from the clutch switch I31. The engine management control unit M10 is connected, via the CAN line, to the Body Computer M1 and to the instrument panel E50, to manage the "Cruise Control on" warning light.
