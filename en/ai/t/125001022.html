Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the oil vapour recovery pipe from the hose from the flowmeter to the air chamber.

2. Loosen the bands (2a) and remove the hose from the flow meter to the air chamber (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Refit in its seating the rigid hose from the flow meter to the air chamber complete with hoses, and tighten the bands.

Connect the oil vapour recovery pipe to the hose from the flow meter to the air chamber.
