0010T64 BRAKE AND OR CLUTCH FLUID CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP AS NECESSARY

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Check that the brake/clutch fluid in the reservoir is at the maximum level; if this is not the case, top up using the recommended fluid.

Avoid the brake/clutch fluid coming into contact with the paintwork; if it does happen, wash immediately with water.

If the level is topped up, remove the reservoir cap without disconnecting the minimum level warning light switch electrical connection.
