Problems encountered

Operation

INTAKE AND EXHAUST SYSTEM SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the exhaust system by looking and listening. In particular, check that there are no leaks from the head outlet to the downstream Lambda sensor

Move on to Step 3

There are leaks

Restore the correct seal for the exhaust system, if necessary, replace the components involved

Problems encountered

Operation

Move on to Step 4

The activation does not take place (or the signal does not switch)
