Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Press the clutch pedal, then refit the clutch pedal switch and turn through 90 degrees anticlockwise until it clicks home on the detent ridge.

Release the clutch pedal and pull back to the end of its travel (rest position).

If the switch is not fully back, it could hold the clutch pedal forward and prevent the hydraulic clutch circuit filling properly.

Press the clutch pedal through its full travel to allow the switch to settle.

Connect the electrical connection to the switch on the clutch pedal.

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
