Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Working under the left side of the dashboard trim, undo the bolts securing the fuse cover.

2. Release the top retainer (2a) then the bottom retainers (2b).

3. Recover the fuse cover.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the fuse cover is not damaged.

Position the cover, engaging the retainers.

Tighten the bolts fixing the cover.
