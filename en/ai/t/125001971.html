Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the cover (1a) using a thin flat screwdriver inserted in the relevant slot (1b).

Take care not to damage the bumper surface.

2. Disengage the retainer from the hole and save the cover.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the lid is undamaged.

Fit the retainer in the hole.

Fit the cover in its seating.
