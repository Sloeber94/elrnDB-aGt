With the ignition key in the ON position, the fuel gauge indicator is not working properly (it indicates a different level from what is actually in the tank)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket.

Check that there are no faults in the Body Computer.

Check the plausibility of the level reading

End of diagnosis

Sensor with broken circuit, sensor short circuited, value inconsistent/implausible in relation to actual fuel level.

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keeping the Examiner connected to the diagnostic socket, check the operation of the pointer in active diagnosis

Continue to Step 3

The pointer does not move

Panel defective, replace the instrument panel Op.

Op. 5560B11 ANALOGUE CONTROL PANEL - R+R OR REPLACE

3

FUEL LEVEL SENSOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the fuel level sensor.

Continue to Step 4

Sensor not working

Replace the fuel pump assembly Op.

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GUAGE CONTROL - R + R

4

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity/insulation of the cable between the fuel level sensor and the Body Computer See

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 5

Cables broken/not insulated

Restore the cable continuity/insulation: See

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace the Body Computer Op.

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R R
