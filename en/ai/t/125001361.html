Connect the recovery device (pipe and reservoir) to the brake caliper bleed valve.

Operate the brake pedal until the brake/clutch reservoir is drained.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the brake fluid minimum level warning light switch.

5. Release the A.B.S. pipes from the fastenings.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

The letters indicating to which caliper and to which brake pump outlet they should be connected are stamped on the control unit at each outlet.RR = to the right rear brake (pipe marked with light blue band)LR = to the left rear brake (pipe marked with green band)RF = to the right front caliper (pipe marked with yellow band)LF = to the left front caliper (pipe marked with white band)MC1 = from the main circuit pump/flange side (pipe marked with black band or pink band when the pipe polyammide resin is black)MC2 = from the secondary circuit pump/front (pipe marked with brown band).

Place the A.B.S. hydraulic and electronic control unit in position and secure it using the clips.

Connect the electrical connection to the VDC system pressure sensor.

1. Connect the rigid pipes from the hydraulic control unit to the brake caliper (1a) hydraulic control unit side and tighten the connectors (1b) to the recommended torque.
