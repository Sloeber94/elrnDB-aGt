Removing

Refitting

1. Raise the armrest.

2. Remove the cover, in the direction of the arrows.

3. Undo the armrest retaining bolts.

4. Remove the armrest.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the armrest is not damaged.

Refit the armrest and secure it with the bolt.

Fit the cover.

Lower the armrest.
