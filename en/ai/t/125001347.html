Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Proceed with the removal

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL R R.

Remove the protective cap from the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

Connect the recovery device (pipe and reservoir) to the brake caliper bleed valve.

Loosen the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

Operate the brake pedal until the brake/clutch reservoir is drained.

Refitting

1. Undo the cover for the brake fluid reservoir.

2. Draw off the brake fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

3. Undo the bolts fixing the brake fluid reservoir.

5. Keep the brake fluid reservoir.
