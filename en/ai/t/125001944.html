Refitting

Removing

Check that the squab cover is intact and shows no signs of damage.

Fit the cover in position and using the special tool, clamp the rings anchoring the cover to the padding.

Name

Country

-

Pliers for metal points

1.878.017.001

Fit the Velcro strips.

Tilt the squab.

1. Position the cover with padding (1b) on the frame (1a).

2. Fit the catch in its seating on the cover.

3. Tighten the catch retaining bolts.

4. Fit the cover along the whole perimeter.

5. Beat in the groove using a rubber hammer.

The use of other tools could damage the cover.

Mould the squab cover into shape.

6. Connect the trim to the seat belt.

7. Tighten the bolts.

8. Fit the squab mounting bracket in position and fix it with the bolts.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Rear seat with central winder

Position the top cover and fix it with the retaining buttons.
