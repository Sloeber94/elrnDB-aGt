Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: Xenon Gas headlamps

Check the insulation to 12 V or the earth of the clock spring lead

Check the contintuity of the clock spring lead in the joint section to the two headlamp alignment motors

Check the insulation of the supply lead (+) to earth (pin 6 headlamp alignment control unit)

Check the continuity of the wiring between the headlamp alignment motors and the headlamp alignment correction control unit
