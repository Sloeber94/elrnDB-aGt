Refitting

Removing

Thoroughly remove the sealant residues from the rear bearing cap seat, the small holes and the contact surfaces with the oil sump.

Thoroughly remove all traces of engine oil using heptane or a similar solvent.

1. Place the rear bearing cap (1a), complete with half-bearing, back in its housing and secure it tightening the nuts (1b) to the recommended torque.

The safety notches on the crankcase and main bearing caps must all be on the same side.
