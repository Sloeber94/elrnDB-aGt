U01 FUEL ODOURS FROM THE BOOT

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the car in motion, a smell is noted coming from the rear of the car, particularly after refuelling

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First of all, check the fuel tank for leaks or damage

Continue to Step 1

Leaks from the fuel tank

Repair the fuel tank, or replace if necessary Op.

Op. 1040A14 FUEL TANK - R + R FOR REPLACEMENT

1

CHECK AIR OUTLET FITTINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rear air outlet fittings In particular, ascertain:

  • Fin position (they should open outward)
  • Correct fitting

Continue to Step 2

Incorrect fitting

Restore the fittings to the correct position Op.

Op. 5010C60 LH OR RH REAR AIR OUTLET -ONE- R R

2

CHECK TANK VENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the tank vent is positioned correctly

Continue to Step 3

Vent position incorrect

Reposition vent correctly Op. 1040A50

3

CHECK TANK FILLER FITTING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tank filler fitting, in particular:

  • The seal of the filler housing on the wing;
  • The condition of the filler;
  • The cap seal

Continue to Step 4

Filler or cap faulty

Repair filler Op.

Op. 1040A30 OIL SEAL COVER, FLY WHEEL SIDE - R + R AND REPLACE SEALS

or cap

4

CHECK PUMP COVER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check fuel pump/gauge cover gasket seal

Continue to Step 5

Gasket worn, cover damaged

Replace the gasker or cover Op.

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GUAGE CONTROL - R + R

5

VAPOUR RECOVERY SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seal of the vapour recovery system

Continue to Step 6

Leaks from vapour recovery system

Tighten connections, clips. If necessary, replace the components involved

6

CHECK BOOT FRAME SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that boot is sealed correctly.

Continue to Step 7

Boot seal faulty

Restore correct boot seal

7

UNDERBODY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the underbody plugs in the boot area are present

Continue to Step 8

Underbody plugs missing

Refit the missing plugs

8

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
