Applies to: all engines; all production periods

WARNINGS

Work carefully on the fittings and use the recommended tools correctly to prevent damage.

Take care to avoid any damage to the body paintwork.

PRECAUTIONS

Before working on the electrical system (connectors, electrical components, wiring, etc.) turn the ignition key OFF and DISCONNECT THE BATTERY.

Whenever the battery terminal leads are disconnected, run the window control unit initialisation procedure (right and left sides) to store the door glass end travel, refer to

Op. 7005M40 FRONT ELECTRIC WINDOW SYSTEM ECU - R+R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK
