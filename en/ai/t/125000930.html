Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and (1b), then remove the flywheel side oil seal cover (1c) with the built in oil seal.

Cut the sealant between the oil sump and the flywheel side oil seal cover.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Apply silicone sealant to the matching surfaces between the oil sump and the flywheel side oil seal cover.

1. Place the flywheel side oil cover with the built-in oil seal (1a) back in its housing using the tool (1b).

Removing

Refitting

Remove the flywheel side oil seal from its housing.

Take care not to damage the oil seal seat during removal.The operation of removing the oil seal destroys it, therefore it must always be replaced.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place a new flywheel side oil seal (1a) back in its housing using the tool (1b).

The oil seal should be fitted in its housing so that it covers the small openings (1c).
