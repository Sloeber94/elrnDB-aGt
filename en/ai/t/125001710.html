Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRECAUTIONS

Before working on the electrical system (connectors, electrical components, wiring, etc.) turn the ignition off (STOP) and DISCONNECT THE BATTERY.

Use care when working on trim components and use the specified tools correctly in order to avoid damage.

WARNINGS

When handling the speakers, observe the following precautions:

  • Avoid stacking the speakers on one another.
  • Avoid placing the speakers near ferrous metal parts which could get into the gap and produce noises.
  • Avoid twisting the terminals once the electrical connection is fitted.
  • Avoid touching and damaging the speaker membrane and cone.
  • Prevent the speaker from coming into contact with solvents or other chemical products.
  • Handle the speakers with care using the structure without touching the membrane and other moving parts.
  • Check that the speakers are not damaged or distorted.
  • Remove any protection before fitting.
