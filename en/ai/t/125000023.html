Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fuel consumption figures given in the table have been determined in accordance with the recommendations in EUROPEAN directives.

These figures have been defined during the course of the homologation tests.

The test conditions include:

  • an urban cycle which includes a cold start followed by a varied, simulated urban cycle;
  • an extra-urban cycle which includes frequent acceleration, in all gears, simulating normal out of town usage of the vehicle. The speed is between 0 and 120 km/h;
  • The combined consumption is determined by taking around 37% of the urban cycle consumption and around 63% of the extra-urban cycle consumption.

The type of route, traffic conditions, driving style, weather conditions, trim level/equipment, accessories, presence of a roof rack or special equipment and the state of the vehicle in general can lead to fuel consumption figures that differ from those obtained.
