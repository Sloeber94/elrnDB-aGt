1. Loosen the bolts retaining the timing belt moving tensioner.

2. Release and remove the toothed timing drive belt.

Instructions for checking the condition of the toothed timing belt correctly:

check that the belt does not show signs of fraying or cracking;

check for black powder residues (due to belt rubbing) along the length of the belt, on the toothed wheels, on the guards etc.;

check the belt teeth sides to see whethe the outer protective cloth has worn.

Replace the timing belt if one or more of the above indicators of excessive wear are present. .

Inspect the belt along its full length.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods
