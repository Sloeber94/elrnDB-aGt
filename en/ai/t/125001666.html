Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting
  • Open the bonnet.
  • Disconnect the negative battery terminal.
  • Release the retainers (1a) and remove the cover (1b).
  • Disconnect the electrical connection.
  • Release the retaining spring moving it sideways.
  • Extract the bulb from the lamp.

If the bulb is working properly, avoid touching it with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing
  • Check the condition of the bulb and make sure that it is clean.

Avoid touching the bulb with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

  • Fit the lamp bulb positioning it as illustrated in the diagram.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

  • Attach the retaining spring.
  • Connect the electrical connection.
  • Position the cover and attach the retainers.
  • Connect the negative battery terminal.
  • Close the bonnet.
  • Check that the bulb is working.
