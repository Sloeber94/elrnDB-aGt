It is not possible to order a single lock barrel or to order barrels simply by using a part code. When ordering barrels, observe the special procedure set out in 5580E - Anti-theft device.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Release the retaining spring for the key lock barrel.

2. Fit the key in the lock barrel and extract it from the handle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the barrel is undamaged.

Insert the key in the barrel.

Insert the barrel in its housing.

Engage the retaining clip on the barrel.

After fitting the handle, check that the barrel and the lock control mechanism are working properly.
