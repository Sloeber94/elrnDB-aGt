Position the vehicle on the ramp

Proceed with removal

Op. 4450B11 TYRE INFLATION VALVE CAP - REPLACE

1. Undo the nut (1a) of the bolt and move the rear suspension front trailing arm (1b) as much as necessary.

2. Undo the nut on the top bolt securing the shock absorber to the hub vertical link.

This operation enables you to access the bottom bolt securing the brake caliper bracket.

3. Undo the bolts (3a), move the bracket (3b) complete with rear brake caliper (3c) after releasing the flexible pipe from the attachment on the shock absorber, then secure it appropriately.

This operation enables you to access the top bolt securing the brake caliper bracket.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and remove the rear brake disc (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
