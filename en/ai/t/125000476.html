SENSOR REPLACEMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

If there is a gear engagement sensor error and no faults are detected at the connections/wiring, replace it Op. 2127E42

and check that the position reading is correct by carrying out a series of C.M.

In some cases, when the sensor is dismantled, there may be iron filings deposited on the sensor head

It is advisable to fit tool n° 1870753001 before removing the potentiometer from the hydraulic unit

If there is a gear selector sensor error and no faults are detected at the connections/wiring, replace it Op. 2127E40

and check that the position reading is correct by carrying out a series of C.M.

If there is a clutch disc speed error and no faults are detected at the connections/wiring, replace it Op. 2127E52

and check that the position reading is correct by carrying out a series of C.M.

The sensor is located on the gearbox casing to the right of the reversing lights sensor
