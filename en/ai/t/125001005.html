1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GUAGE CONTROL - R + R

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

.

If the submerged pump assembly complete with levle gauge is being scrapped, the fuel must always be drained following the instructions described below:whilst extracting the submerged pump assembly draw off the fuel in the chamger using an (electric or manual) pump in line;completely extract the submerged pump assembly and, working on a special workbench, connect an (electrical or manual) in line pump to the connector;activate the pump for the time required to collect most of the fuel inside the filter.

Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal.

When scrapping the submerged pump assembly with level gauge mechanism, always empty of fuel as follows:While removing the submerged pump assembly, draw off any fuel in the tank using an in-line pump (electric or manual);fully remove the submerged pump assembly then, working at the bench, connect an in-line pump (electric or manual) to the assembly connector;Run the pump for as long enough to collect most of the petrol present inside the filter.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods
