Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

It is not possible to remove/refit the manual gearbox with differential in the car. The power unit has to be removed/refitted and then the manual gearbox with differential has to be separated from the engine at the bench.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods
