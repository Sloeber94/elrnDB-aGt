SPECIFCATIONS

GENERAL VIEW

1 - Hub

2 - Multipolar magnetic ring

3 - Suspension stub axle pin

CONSTRUCTION FEATURES

The rear wheel hubs consist of an outer wheel fixing flange and a wheel bearing. The hubs are attached to the suspension stub axle pins by nuts with a safety crown. The outer flange also acts as an outer cage for the bearing. A multi-pole magnetic ring is fitted to the hub's outer flange. Its function is to indicate wheel movement to the active sensor of the ABS

Characteristic of working principle 3340 ANTI-LOCK BRAKE SYSTEM (ABS)

.

When dismantling, ALWAYS use a new nut to secure the hub and stake the crown.
