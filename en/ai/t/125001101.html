Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp

1. Loosen the collar fixing the front exhaust silencer to the rear exhaust silencer.

2. Remove the rear exhaust silencer (2a) after having released it from the flexible mountings (2b) and from the front exhaust silencer.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the rear exhaust silencer back in its housing, connect it to the front exhaust silencer and secure it using the flexible mountings.

Tighten the collar fixing the front exhaust silencer to the rear exhaust silencer.

Remove the car from the ramps.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp

1. Loosen the collar securing the rear exhaust silencer to the rigid exhaust pipe.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Remove the rear exhaust silencer after having released it from the flexible mountings and from the rigid exhaust pipe.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the rear exhaust silencer back in its housing, connect it to the rigid exhaust pipe and secure it using the flexible mountings.

Tighten the collar fastening the intermediate exhaust silencer to the rigid exhaust pipe.

Remove the car from the ramps.
