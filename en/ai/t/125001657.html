5540B44 DIPPED BEAM FOR ONE GAS DISCHARGE LIGHTING UNIT ONE LH OR RH R R

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK; all production periods

Remove the bulb without the headlamp connected because the bulb reaches a voltage of 20,000 V at the moment of activation and a voltage of several hundred volts is present at the bulb terminals during normal operation.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Op. 5540B76 FRONT LIGHT CLUSTER WITH FOGLAMP ONE LH OR RH R R WITH INTERFERING PARTS REMOVED

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the protective bulb cover (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Rotate the bulb holder in an anti-clockwise direction (bayonet fitting) and extract it.

2. Rotate the ring nut in an anti-clockwise direction and extract it.

3. Extract the gas discharge dipped headlamp bulb.

If the lamp is working properly, do not touch the bulb with your hands; if this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check the condition and the cleanliness of the lamp.

Do not touch the bulb with your hands; if this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

1. Place the bulb (1a) in the housing, inserting the seats (1b) and (1b) on the teeth (1d) and (1e), respectively.

2. Place the ring nut (2a) in position, with the word TOP (2b) upwards and also ensure that the housings (2c) are aligned with the bulb pins (2d); lastly, rotate the ring nut in the housing.

3. Fit the bulb holder and rotate it into position (bayonet fitting).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Place the protective bulb cover in position and secure it using the bolts.
