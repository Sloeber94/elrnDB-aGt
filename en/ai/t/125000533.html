I05 DIPPED HEADLAMPS NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the stalk unit collar is operated with the key turned on, the dipped headlamps do not work.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

In the case of vehicles with gas discharge headlamps, it is possible that both bulbs and the control units are broken simultaneously. It is fairly improbable that there would be faults in both lamps simultaneously, therefore the possibility of problems with the Body Computer should be considered first. In rare cases there could be simultaneous problems with the bulbs, control units and Body Computer.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
