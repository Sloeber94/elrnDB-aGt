0010T65 HYDRAULIC BRAKE SYSTEM BLEED AIR

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Cnnect the tool to the brake/clutch fluid reservoir.

Description

Code

1

Brake - clutch bleed equipment

1.806.097.001

2. Remove the protective cap from the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

3. Connect the recovery device (pipe and reservoir) to the brake caliper bleed valve.

4. Open the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

5. Open the fluid reservoir tap slowly.

Wait for all the air in the hydraulic system to escape.

Close the bleed valve on the brake callipers and fit the protective plug.

Repeat the operations described above for the remaining brake calipers.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Remove the recovery device (pipe and reservoir)

Remove the equipment.
