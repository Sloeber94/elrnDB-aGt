Before removing and refitting the pretensioners, the following preliminary operations must be carried out: turn the ignition off ('STOP') and remove the key; disconnect the battery leads and insulate them; wait for 10 minutes before continuing. FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY RULES FOR AIR BAGS (see 5580C - Warnings and precautions).
