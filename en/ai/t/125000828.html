0010M20 120,000KM SERVICE COUPON, EMISSION CONTROL VERSIONS

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Check the tyre tread is not irregularly worn.

Some types of tyre are fitted with wear gauges; they should be replaced as soon as these gauges are visible on the tread.

Do not swap tyres across.

Check the tyre inflation pressure is correct. Also check the spare wheel.

The tyre pressures must be checked when they are cold.

Check that the lighting system is operating correctly: headlamps, turn signals, hazard lights, luggage compartment light, passenger compartment light, oddment compartment light and instrument panel warning lights.

Check the windscreen wiper system is operating correctly and adjust the sprays if necessary.

Check the front and rear wiper blades are correctly positioned and not worn.

Check the front disc brake pad wear indicator is operating correctly.

Check the condition of the rear disc brake pads and ensure they are not worn.

Carry out a visual check of:

  • body exterior
  • underbody protection
  • rigid and flexible pipe sections (exhaust - fuel system - brakes)
  • rubber components (gaiters, sleeves, bushes etc.)

After the service, connect the Examiner tester to the tester output on the vehicle and establish a connection with the control unit "MANNESMAN VDO INSTRUMENT PANEL".

Before carrying active diagnosis "INSPECTION SERVICE VALUE" check the instrument panel UNIT menu to see whether the value is set to km or Miles according to the measurement unit in the relevant country.

While in Active Diagnosis mode, run the INSPECTION SERVICE VALUE procedure to store/update the mileage level at which the service was carried out.

Check on the instrument panel SERVICE menu that in countries that use the Km measurement unit, the number of kilometres displaced corresponds to 20,000 while for countries that use Miles, the number of miles displayed corresponds to 12,427.
