1. Undo the front screws securing the ceiling trim.

2. Remove the door seals near the ceiling trim.

3. Release the inner Velcro retainers.

4. Remove the roof lining and extract it from the passenger compartment through the tailgate housing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the cover is undamaged.

Insert the ceiling lining into the passenger compartment through the tailgate opening.

Position the ceiling trim and engage the inner Velcro retainers.

Tighten the front screws securing the ceiling trim.

Fit the door compartment seals.
