Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the central locking is operated using either the key or the remote control or from the inside using the safety knob, one of the front doors does not close properly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

If one door does not close properly, both doors may close and then re-open after a few moments when the door lock is operated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
