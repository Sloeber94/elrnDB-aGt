I06 MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the dipped headlamps switched on (stable position) or in the flashing mode, when the steering column switch unit lever is operated, the main beam headlamps do not work.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS RELAY CHECK (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Swap the main beam headlamps relay (in the engine compartment junction unit) with the horn relay (the middle one in the same row) and check that the main beam headlamps are working properly after the swap

Continue to Step 2

The main beam headlamps still do not work even after the swap

Continue to Step 3

2

REPLACING HORN RELAY (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Replace the horn relay (which, in reality, was the one for the main beam headlamps) and check that it is working properly

End of diagnosis

.

3

MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS POWER SUPPLY CHECK (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Refit the two relays in their original positions.

With the ignition ON, check that the main beam headlamps relay is correctly supplied by the battery.

Then connect to the relay supply from the Body Computer and check that when the main beam headlamps are activated, the supply is sent to the relay.

Also check that the electrical connections between the battery and the relay are not faulty See

See E2012 MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS

Do not connect the relay

Continue to Step 4

No supply from battery

Restore the correct supply to the relay. If necessary replace the faulty components (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Supply enablement not provided by the Body Computer

Continue to Step 6

Connections faulty

Restore the correct connections.

4

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket.

Check:

  • That there are no faults in the Body Computer
  • The operation of the lights through active diagnosis

MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Left and right main beam headlamp fuses;
  • Connection between steering column switch unit and Body Computer;
  • Connection between Body Computer and engine compartment control unit
  • Connections between engine bay control unit and bulbs;
  • Bulb earth SeeSee E2012 MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS

Continue to Step 7

Blown fuses

Replace the fuses, then search for the cause of the problem

Connections not correct

Restore the connections See

See E2012 MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS

Lamps earth defective

Restore the earth to the bulbs See

See E2012 MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS
