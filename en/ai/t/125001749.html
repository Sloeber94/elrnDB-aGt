Removing

Refitting
  • Open the bonnet.
  • Disconnect the negative battery terminal.
  • Work carefully.

Follow the instructions for handling speakers.Op. 5570 ACCESSORIES

  • Op. 7040L40 REAR COMPARTMENT SCUFF STRIP - R R
  • Op. 7050A22 REAR SEAT CUSHION - R+R
  • Op. 7040H50 REAR PANEL - R R
  • Disconnect the electrical connection.
  • Undo the bolts.
  • Keep the speaker.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing
  • Check that the speaker is not damaged.
  • Position the speaker.
  • Tighten the bolts.
  • Connect the electrical connection.
  • Connect the negative battery terminal.
  • Close the bonnet.
  • Check that the speaker is working.

  • Op. 7050A22 REAR SEAT CUSHION - R+R
