Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Open the rear tailgate.

1. Release the rear parcel shelf from the tailgate releasing the retaining rings.

2. Extract the rear parcel shelf (2a) releasing it from the side support pins (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the rear parcel shelf inserting it in the side support pins.

Secure the rear parcel shelf to the tailgate attaching the retaining rings.
