5530B40 SUPPLY BOX ON BATTERY (LINK BATTERY AND FUSE BOX) - R R

Refitting

Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal (-).

Refitting

Removing

Place the casing on the battery in position and tighten the terminal bolt.

Place the retaining bracket in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

Connect the electrical connections to the casing on the battery.

Place the cover for the casing on the battery in position.

Connect the negative battery terminal (-).
