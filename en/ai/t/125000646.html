When the centre of the steering wheel cover is pressed, the horns do not work or they sound out of tune

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

HORNS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the horns.

Continue to Step 2

Horns faulty.

Repair the horns. Op.

Op. 5550B30 L/H OR R/H HORN (ONE) - R+R

Horns working out of tune

Replace the horns Op.

Op. 5550B30 L/H OR R/H HORN (ONE) - R+R

2

HORNS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Horns fuse
  • Horns relay
  • Horns earth
  • Continuity and insulation of electrical circuit between dashboard control unit and horns SeeSee E3010 HORNS

Continue to Step 3

Defective component

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore the connections See

See E3010 HORNS

3

CLOCK SPRING/STEERING WHEEL ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Connection between switches on steering wheel and clock spring.
  • Continuity and insulation of electrical circuit between clock spring and dashboard control unit
  • Operation of the switches
  • Operation of the clock spring SeeSee E3010 HORNS

Continue to Step 4

Connections not correct

Restore the connections See

See E3010 HORNS

Clock spring faulty

Replace the clock spring Op.

Op. 5580C12 CLOCK SPRING FOR AIR BAG MODULE ON STEERING WHEEL R R

Switches faulty

Replace faulty component
