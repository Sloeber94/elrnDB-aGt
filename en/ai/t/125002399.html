Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Description

The radiator and air conditioner condenser cooling system consists of two electric fans operated in parallel at two different speeds:

  • the first speed is activated at an initial engine coolant temperature level or a certain air conditioning refrigerant fluid pressure;
  • the second speed is engaged at a higher temperature or pressure.

The fans are operated by means of three specific relays located near the engine compartment junction unit.

The supply line for the fans is protected by two special MAXIFUSES.
