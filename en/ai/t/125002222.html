Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Functional description

The system for the horns is supplied through the horn relay T3 of the engine compartment control unit

B1

from the direct line from the battery through the fuse F10 located in

B1

.

The relay coil is energized by an earth signal when one of the steering switches is closed; these switches are connected via the clock spring to the stalk unit

H5

. The relay thereby supplies the high tone horn

P10

and the low tone horn

P11

.

The connection for the horn contact is via the clock spring connector

D47

, which also transmits the signals for the driver's Air Bag and the steering wheel controls (for radio and telephone)

See E3510 RADIO

See E7030 AIRBAG

.
