CONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

In addition to its normal function of covering the cylinder head, the aluminium tappet cover also acts as a separator/condenser for the engine oil vapour recovery system.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

CONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

The tappet covers are made from aluminium, the one on the right is incorporated with the air chamber.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

CONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

The nylon tappet cover incorporates the oil vapour separator
