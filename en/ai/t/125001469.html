  • Position the vehicle on a test bench, with sliding plates on all the wheels.
  • Arrange the vehicle in standard A load condition (empty + full tank of fuel)
  • Check that the tyre inflation pressure is correct.

TOE IN CHECK

  • Check that the front wheel toe in is within required limits.

Measurement

Value

-

Front wheel toe in D2 - D1 (mm)

A vehicle unladen with full tanks (standard A)

- 1 ± 0.6

The front wheel toe in angle is - 9’ ± 5’.

The toe in value, is calculated using a 15" wheel rim irrespective of the wheel size actually fitted; if it is not possible to use a 15" wheel on the test bench, refer to the original values.It should also be remembered that the toe in value includes the tolerance that should be equally distributed between the two wheels.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

  • If the front wheel toe in value is not within the recommended figures, adjust it following the instructions given below.
  • Make sure that the steering wheel is perfectly straight and lock it in this position using a suitable tool.
  • Loosen the bands securing the power assisted steering box boots to the steering rods.
  • Loosen the steering rod nuts.
  • Tighten or loosen the steering rods until the toe in is correct keeping the front wheels aligned.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

  • Tighten the steering rod nuts to the recommended torque.
