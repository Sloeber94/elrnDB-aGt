Removing

Refitting

Release the rear seat backrest and tilt it forwards to allow the subsequent extraction of the head restraint.

1. Raise the head restraint to the limit of its travel.

2. Press the retainers and withdraw the head restraint.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the head restraint is undamaged.

Refit the head restraint fully home in its seating.

Press the retainers and insert the head restraint.

Attach the rear seat backrest to the striker.

Check the operation of the rear head restraint height adjustment.
