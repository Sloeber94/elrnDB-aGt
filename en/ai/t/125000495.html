Operation

Check, in order:

  • The condition of the air intake circuit;
  • That there are no faults in the air filter
  • The seal of the diesel pipes
  • That there is no water, petrol, paraffin or dirt in the fuel tank and filter
  • That the battery earth is correctly fixed
  • That there no obstructions in the exhaust system
