Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle stationary, the gear lever in neutral and the engine running, there is a high frequency, low intensity noise in the area of the gearbox. The fault is often accompanied by an oil leak from the gearbox: also see [DI_X015]

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

  • No damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring;
