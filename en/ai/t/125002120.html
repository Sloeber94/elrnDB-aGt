Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The dipped beam activation signal is sent to pin 4 of connector D of the Body Computer

M1

from the stalk unit

H5

.

The 'ignition ON' signal (INT) for the switch H1 reaches the underdashboard control unit

B2

at pin 11 of connector H and from there moves on to the Body Computer

M1

(pin 9 of connector F).

The Body Computer

M1

controls the supply of the dipped headlamps sending an earth signal to the underdashboard control unit

B2

- from pin 8 of connector F of M1 - which energizes the relay T1: this supplies the dipped headlamps

F10

(left) and

F11

(right). Each circuit is protected by a control unit

B2

fuse: F13 for the left headlamp and F12 for the right one.
