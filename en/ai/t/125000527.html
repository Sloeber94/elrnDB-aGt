H02 DRIFT WHILST BRAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During braking, the car pulls to the right and/or left

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK FOR OIL LEAKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there are no fluid leaks from:

  • calipers;
  • master cylinder - condition of braking system

Continue to Step 2

Fluid leak from a front caliper

Replace the faulty caliper Op.

Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER ONE R R

Fluid leak from a rear caliper

Replace the faulty caliper Op.

Op. 3310B36 REAR BRAKE CALLIPER - R R

2

CHECK ON BRAKE WEAR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the state of wear of the brakes - condition of brake surfaces

Continue to Step 3

Brakes very worn

Overhaul the brakes

Discs vitrified or with traces of grease

Clean and check for causes

3

CHECK ON WHEEL GEOMETRY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the geometry and track of the wheel axle

Continue to Step 4

Front wheel geometry outside limits

Restore the correct front wheel geometry Op.

Op. 4450A10 FRONT WHEEL GEOMETRY - CHECK USING OPTICAL EQUIPMENT - INCLUDES FRONT WHEEL TOW-IN ADJUSTMENT IF NECESSARY

Rear wheel geometry incorrect

Restore the correct rear wheel geometry Op.

Op. 4450A40 REAR WHEEL GEOMETRY - CHECK USING OPTICAL EQUIPMENT

4

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check that there are no faults in the ABS control unit

Continue to Step 5

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector See E7020 ABS

5

CHECK ON DRIFT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Disconnect the ABS control unit.

Check whether the vehicle still drifts during braking at variable speeds

Continue to Step 6

Vehicle does not drift during braking

Replace ABS control unit Op.

Op. 3340A20 ABS ELECTRONIC CONTROL UNIT - R+R

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Test the vehicle before delivery to the customer, checking that the fault is no longer present

The test is essential, as the braking system is a safety feature of the car

End of diagnosis
