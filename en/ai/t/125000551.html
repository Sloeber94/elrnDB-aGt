I60 FLASHING, SWITCHING OFF OR VARIATION IN DIPPED HEADLAMPS BRIGHTNESS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Flashing, switching off or irregular variation in the brightness of the dipped headlamp in one or both of the light clusters

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Warning: Presence of high voltage (25kV) at the gas discharge bulb connector during the first 0.7 secs after the dipped headlamps have been turned on.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Do not switch on the dipped headlamps when the discharge bulb connector is not correctly connected.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Do not disconnect the discharge bulb connector with the dipped headlamps switched on.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Handle the discharge bulb with clean, cloth gloves and never touch the glass bulb with your fingers.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Do not reverse the polarity of the lamp supply lines or else the control unit will be irreversably damaged.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
