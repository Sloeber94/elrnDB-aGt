Applies to: all engines; all production periods

WARNINGS

Use care when working on trim components and use the specified tools correctly in order to avoid damage.

Take care to avoid any damage to body paintwork.

PRECAUTIONS

Before working on the electrical system (connectors, electrical components, wiring, etc.) turn the ignition off (STOP) and DISCONNECT THE BATTERY.

When working on the seat belt pretensioners, refer to the precautions for the Air Bags

Characteristic of working principle 5580C AIR BAG SYSTEM
