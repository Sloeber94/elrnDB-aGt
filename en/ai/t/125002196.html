Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

According to the battery voltage control logic described previously, the Body Computer

M1

supplies the luggage compartment light G40 from pin 33 of connector E and also the sun visor lights - driver's side

G31

and passenger side

G32

; the glove compartment light

G30

is supplied in the same way from pin 16 of connector C.

The boot lid opening switch

I11

sends an earth signal with the boot open and lights up the courtesy light

G40

. a second contact

I11 is connected to pin 36 of connector E;

this signal lights up the 'doors open' warning light in the instrument panel

E50.

The glove compartment light

G30

is lit up with an earth signal from the glove compartment switch

I15

.

The 'low battery voltage' signal which switches off the courtesy lights if they are on, reaches the Body Computer

M1

via the CAN

line.

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES
