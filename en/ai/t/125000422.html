INTRODUCTION

The style of the car reflects the Alfa Romeo tradition in which all body parts are designed to enhance the car's sporty image. Meanwhile the interor is characterised by soft and harmonious shapes, which create an extremely comfortable environment while retaining a sporty hallmark.

Particular attenton has been given to designing the aerodynamics, using guards, designed in a wind tunnel, to shield the rear suspension trailing arms which, together with the profile of the spare wheel compartment, assist air flow along the underbody.

Thorough study of space distribution and details has ensured that all possible wind noise has been reduced to a minimum.
