REPLACING UPSTREAM LAMBDA SENSORS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Replace the upstream Lambda sensors.

Search for the cause of the pollution in the sensors.

The probable causes are:

  • Contaminated petrol;
  • Engine oil leaks;
  • Improper use of fastenings at sensor connector

Continue to Step 3

Use of contaminated petrol

Clean the tank Op. 1040A24

and introduce suitable quality fuel.
