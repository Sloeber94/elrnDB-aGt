Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The electric windows are not operated when the remote control is used for the super-opening/closing functions.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The super-opening and superclosing functions are implemented by means of a command which travels via the CAN from the Body Computer to the Driver's Door Node and then to the electric window control units. Failed operation could be due to faults in the entire circuit

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
