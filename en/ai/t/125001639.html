Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Follow these precautions.

Do not on any account carry out fast recharging. For correct battery recharing, use a battery charger recommended by the Service Department, e.g. ISEAT CB1): this is a constant voltage battery charger with a microprocessor which recognizes the battery charge and automatically determines the best recharging cycle. This means that the battery will be almost 100% fully charged up.

Always disconnect the battery negative terminal before recharging; while recharging, do not touch the battery terminals using your hands and do not bring naked flames close to the battery; operate the battery charger only after connecting the leads to the battery.

If the measurement of the battery voltage is less than 12.10 V, recharging is not recommended and the battery should be replacedOp. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R.
