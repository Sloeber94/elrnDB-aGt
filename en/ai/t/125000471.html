The vehicle switches to automatic by itself when driving in manual

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

It should be remembered that, keeping the lever in a position other than the rest position (e.g. if the driver is in the habit of keeping their hand on the lever), will result in switching from manual to City after 12 secconds
