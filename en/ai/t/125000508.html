CHECK ON RADIATOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the radiator. In particular, check for signs of deformation - traces of leaks - external dirt - fluid with excessive traces of rust.

Continue to Step 3

Radiator distorted

Replace the radiator Op.

Op. 1088B20 ENGINE COOLING RADIATOR - R + R

Radiator dirty

Clean the radiator Op. 1088A20

Radiator is leaking

Replace the radiator Op.

Op. 1088B20 ENGINE COOLING RADIATOR - R + R

Liquid with excessive traces of rust

Clean the inside of the radiator and cooling circuit

Fan not working

Activate the fan motor directly, replace if it does not work, check the following in order if it does work: specific fuses and relays on the engine bay control unit, condition of 1st speed resistor, specific wiring continuity and insulation. Check that the control unit activates the fan speed relays. If not, replace the i.e. control unit. If it does, replace the engine bay control unit
