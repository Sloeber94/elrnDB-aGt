Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

It remains stuck in one gear and, when the vehicle stops, the engine cuts out and cannot be started up again or it starts again after a few minutes.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Preliminary check: If during engine starting you notice that the starter motor works normally, the problem is caused by other systems (engine management, ABS, fuel pump). Refer to specific charts.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

If the starter motor does not turn (after checking its operation and the relevant electrical supply circuit), continue with the next diagnosis chart.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods
