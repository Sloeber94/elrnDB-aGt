Removing

Refitting

1. If necessary, undo the bolts (1a) and remove clutch hydraulic actuator (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Clean the mating surfaces between the clutch hydraulic actuator and the gearbox of sealant residues.

Apply silicon sealant to the matching surfaces of the hydraulic clutch actuator and the gearbox.

Place the hydraulic clutch actuator in position and secure it tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.
