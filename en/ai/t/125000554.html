I65 DIPPED HEADLAMPS LOWERD AND NOT MOVING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Both headlamps are lowered and not moving.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Warning: Presence of high voltage (25kV) at the gas discharge bulb connector during the first 0.7 seconds after the dipped headlamps have been turned on.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Do not switch on the dipped headlamps when the discharge lamp connector is not correctly connected.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Do not disconnect the discharge bulb connector with the dipped headlamps switched on.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Handle the discharge bulb with clean, cloth gloves and never touch the glass bulb with your fingers.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Do not reverse the polarity of the supply line for the headlamps or else irreversible damage will be caused to the control unit.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
