Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from the area of the dashboard.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a road test to check there is no noise.

If there is noise, check that it actually comes from the area of the dashboard and try and identify the area where the creaking is emanating from. For example, press the various parts described, checking whether the creaking disappears

End of diagnosis

CHECKING STEERING COLUMN COVER FASTENING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column cover is correctly fixed to the dashboard

in particular, make sure that the following are present:

  • insulating material around the cables
  • Felt in the matching area with the dashboard;
  • free movement of the steering wheel height adjustment device
  • steering column cover casing fixings

Continue to Step 6

Fixing faulty

Restore the correct fixing to the dashboard. Refit the felt pads if necessary Op.
