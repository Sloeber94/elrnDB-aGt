With the vehicle moving, there is noise coming from one of the front doors.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Operation

Firstly check the moving window glass is properly closed.

Check that the noise is actually coming from one of the front doors by carrying out a road test for preliminary wind noise location

FRONT DOOR WIND NOISE LOCATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the detailed check for locating wind noise from the front doors See Test

See Test 7005A LOCATION OF FRONT DOOR WIND NOISES

: identify the area where the noise is coming from.

Continue to Step 2

The noise persists with the entire door taped

Check for sealant between the wing and the pillar under the wheel arch

The wind noise is coming from the area between the pillar and the windscreen

Check the condition of the door compartment seal in that area and ensure it is bonded properly to the body, if necessary, replace it Op.

Op. 7005A34 FRONT SIDE DOOR COMPARTMENT SEAL R R

The wind noise is coming from the area between the door and the windscreen

Check the condition of the door compartment seal in that area and ensure it is bonded properly to the body, if necessary, replace it. If necessary, adjust the door hinges Op.

Op. 7005A14 FRONT SIDE DOOR - ADJUST CENTERING AND MOULDING - INCLUDES ADAPTATION OF HINGES AND SEALS ON DOOR COMPARTMENT

The wind noise is coming from the area between the door and the wing

Adjust the door hinges Op.

Op. 7005A14 FRONT SIDE DOOR - ADJUST CENTERING AND MOULDING - INCLUDES ADAPTATION OF HINGES AND SEALS ON DOOR COMPARTMENT

The wind noise is coming from the area between the glass and the door

Check the condition of the glass scraper, if necessary, replace it Op.

Op. 7005D26 FRONT SIDE DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS SCRAPER (OUTER) R R

The wind noise is coming from the mirror

Check the condition of the seal between mirror and door and make sure it is correctly fitted. Adjust or replace the actual mirror Op.

Op. 7005R30 ELECTRICALLY CONTROLLED DOOR MIRROR (ONE) - R+R
