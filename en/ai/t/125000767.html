2127B OIL LEVEL CHECK

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Wait until the pump goes out (The pump is activated when the driver's door is opened and goes out automatically after a maximum of 30 seconds).

Turn the ignition key to the ON position.

Make various gear changes with the engine switched off until the pump is activated.

Immediately after the pump cuts out, check the level of the hydraulic fluid using the dipstick under the cap for the reservoir.

If the fluid level is below the MAX mark, note the amount of fluid required to top up.
