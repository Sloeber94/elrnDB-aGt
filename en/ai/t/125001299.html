1. Remove the two bevels and undo the nut fixing the front wheel hub to the driveshaft.

Use a 36 mm USAG spanner modified as shown in the diagram to loosen the above nut.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Proceed with the removal

Op. 4410A80 DAMPER LOWER FORK - R R.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the driveshaft complete with joints (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing
