Removing

Refitting
  • Op. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R
  • Op. 5530B52 BATTERY DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R.

1. Release the electrical wiring from the mounting bracket.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection from the gear selection solenoid valve 2.

Description

Connector

2

Solenoid valve 2 (gear engagement)

See L52 SOLENOID 2 (GEAR SHIFT)

3. Disconnect the electrical connection from the gear selection solenoid valve 1.

Description

Connector

3

Solenoid valve 1 (gear engagement)

See L51 SOLENOID 1 (GEAR SHIFT)

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox clutch position sensor.

Description

Connector

1

Clutch position potentiometer

See K95 CLUTCH POSITION POTENTIOMETER

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox clutch position sensor (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing
  • Place the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox clutch position sensor back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.
  • Connect the electrical connection to the electro-hydraulic selection gearbox clutch position sensor.
  • Connect the electrical connection to the gear engagement solenoid valve 1.
  • Connect the electrical connection to the gear engagement solenoid valve 2.
  • Secure the electrical wiring to the bracket.
  • Op. 5530B52 BATTERY DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R.
  • Op. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R
