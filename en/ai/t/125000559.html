J01 WINDSCREEN/REARSCREEN WASHERS NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the igntion key in the ON position, when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is operated, the windscreen wash/wipe does not work properly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

If the vehicle is equipped with a rain sensor, the intermittent position actually corresponds to the 'AUTO' position, i.e. depending on the sensitivity determined on the ring nut, the windscreen wiper is operated automatically if rain is detected.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operation

STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT RELAY CHECK (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Swap the steering column switch unit relay (in the junction unit under the dashboard) with the heated rear windscreen relay (they are next to one another) and check that the windscreen wipers and the rearscreen wiper are working properly after the swap

Continue to Step 3

The two devices are still not working even after the swap
