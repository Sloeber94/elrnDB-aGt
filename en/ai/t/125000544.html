Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The luggage compartment lights do not work when the relevant controls are operated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

LIGHT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • The condition of the bulb
  • The condition of the bulb holder

Continue to Step 2

Bulb or courtesy light faulty

Restore or replace the courtesy light Op.

Op. 7040G30 BOOT LIGHT BULB HOLDER - R R

2

LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Body Computer supply
  • Connections between courtesy light and Body Computer;
  • Connection with boot opening switch;
  • Courtesy light earth connection SeeSee E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore the correct connections See

See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

Body Computer earth faulty

Restore the earth connection See

See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

Faulty Body Computer supply

Restore connection See

See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

3

LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT OPENING SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the luggage compartment opening switch and that it is working properly

Continue to Step 4

Boot open switch faulty

Repair or replace the boot opening lock Op.

Op. 7015B10 SINGLE TAILGATE LOCK - R+R OR REPAIR WITH PANEL/PROTECTION REMOVED

4

CHECK ON SIGNAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that when the boot is opened, an earth signal is sent from the Body Computer to the courtesy light

Continue to Step 5

No earth signal from Body Computer

Restore the correct supply to the Body Computer. If necessary, replace the Body Computer Op.

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R R

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
