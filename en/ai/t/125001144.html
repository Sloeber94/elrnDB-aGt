Op. 1072D10 MODULAR INLET MANIFOLD - R+R.

1. Unscrew the bolts securing the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate junction to the solenoid, to the air capacity chamber.

2. Release the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate junction to the solenoid from its fastening.

3. Unscrew the bolts securing the bracket for the solenoid enabling the passage of fuel vapours to the intake.

4. Loosen collar (4a) and remove the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate junction to the solenoid (4b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Refit the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate junction to the solenoid and secure with a collar.

Working at the bench, unscrew the bolts securing the solenoid enabling the passage of fuel vapours to the intake.

Secure the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate junction to the solenoid to its fastening.

Tighten the bolts securing the fuel vapour pipe from the intermediate junction to the solenoid to the air capacity chamber.
