Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

.

The presence of water in the fuel system can cause severe damage to the entire injection system and cause irregular engine operation.

Position the vehicle on the ramp

Proceed with removal

Refer to op.. 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r

Proceed with removal

Disconnect the electrical connection for the water-in-fuel filter sensor.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

Loosen the water-in-fuel filter sensor until there is a flow of only fuel, then retighten the sensor.

Connect the electrical connection to the water-in-fuel filter presence sensor.

Name

Connector

-

Water in diesel filter sensor

See K31 WATER IN DIESEL FILTER SENSOR

Proceed with refitting

Recover the water mixed with diesel in a suitable container.

.

Proceed with refitting

Refer to op.. 1016A10 sound-proofing cover - r + r

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R.

Remove the car from the ramps.
