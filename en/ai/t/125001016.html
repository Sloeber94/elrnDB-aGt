Before fitting a new filter element, remove any impurities in the filter casing (by suction) that could fall into the clean air section of the circuit. Take care to position the filter element correctly so that the gasket is in its housing and is not cut or damaged.

Refitting

Removing

When replacing the air filter, before fitting the new filter element, remove any impurities in the filter casing (by suction) that could fall into the clean air section of the circuit. Ensure that the filter element is correctly positioned so that the seal does not emerge from its housing and is not cut or damaged.

Place a new filter element for the air filter back in the housing.

Place the air filter cover back in its housing and secure with clips.

Refit the air intake hose with air flow meter and secure with collars.

Connect the oil vapour recirculation pipe to the air intake duct.

Connect the electrical connection to the air flow meter and secure the electrical wiring to the fastening on the air intake sleeve.

Connect the battery terminal (-).
