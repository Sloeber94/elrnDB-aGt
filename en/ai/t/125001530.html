Removing

Refitting

Observe the following precaution.

Follow the safety instructions (Op. 5040 AIR CONDITIONING CASING AND COMPONENTS).

- 7025A12 Engine front sound insulation COVER - R.R.

- 7025A14 Bonnet LID - R.R.

Drain the refrigerant (

Op. 0010T84 COOLANT FOR AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM DEHYDRATE AND RECHARGE

).

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

Op. 7055B64 LH OR RH REFLECTOR(ONE) ON REAR BUMPERS - R R

Op. 7055B42 LH OR RH REFLECTOR(ONE) ON REAR BUMPERS - R R

Op. 7210E10 R.R. FRONT BUMPER

Seal the housing for the drier filter cartridge using a suitable plug to prevent dampness and impurities from entering the system.

1. Working in front of the right front wheel, undo the bolts fixing the air duct.

2. Remove the air duct.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Disconnect the pipe connector from the condenser.

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Disconnect the intermediate connector (1a) and the connector for the pipe (1b) from the compressor.

2. Remove the pipe

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged or cracked.

Position the pipe.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors. Only use green coloured seals, resistant to R134A refrigerant. Lubricate the thread of the connectors using anti-freeze.

Connect the pipe connectors and tighten the bolts and the nut.
