Whilst driving, there is a cyclical or whistling noise in a specific gear. This noise decreases or disappears on changing gear

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Whilst driving, steer to the right or the left and check the onset of the noise. Check whether the intensity increases with the steering angle.

Continue to Step 1

Rolling noise which varies with the steering angle both to the left and the right

Replace the wheel hub bearings

Increasing the steering angle produces a noise similar to croaking which varies according to the wheel speed

Move on to Step 2

1

DIFFERENTIAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the differential: In particular, check the bearings

Continue to Step 2

Bearings damaged

Replace the bearings Op.

Op. 2110D26 DIFFERENTIAL INNER CASE BEARINGS - REPLACE WITH DIFFERENTIAL INNER CASE REMOVED

Internal gears worn or damaged

Overhaul or replace the differential housing Op.

Op. 2110D10 DIFFERENTIAL INNER CASE - R + R WITH MANUAL GEARBOX REMOVED - INCLUDES GEARBOX DISMANTLE AND REBUILD

2

WHEEL SIDE CONSTANT VELOCITY JOINT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the wheel side constant velocity joints

Continue to Step 3

Wheel side joint damaged

Replace the wheel side joint Op.

Op. 2710A20 WHEEL END CONSTANT VELOCITY JOINT (ONE), L/H OR R/H - R + R WITH DRIVE SHAFT REMOVED - INCLUDES BOOT REPLACEMENT
