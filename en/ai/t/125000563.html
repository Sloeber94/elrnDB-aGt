With the ignition on, when the heated rear window button is pressed, neither the rear window nor the door mirrors warm up properly

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the heated rear windscreen button in the climate control system control panel is working properly

In particular, check that the defrosting of both the heated rear windscreen and the rear view mirrors is activated and that the warning light in the button in the climate control system control panel comes on

End of diagnosis

HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN RELAY CHECK (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Swap the heated rear windscreen relay (in the junction unit under the dashboard) with the steering column switch unit relay (they are next to one another) and check that the defrosting function is working properly after the swap

Continue to Step 2

The defrosting function still does not work even after the swap

Continue to Step 3

2

REPLACING STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT RELAY (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Replace the steering column switch unit relay (which was actually the one for the rearscreen) and check that the windscreen wipers are working properly

End of diagnosis

.

HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN RELAY POWER SUPPLY CHECK (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the heated rear windscreen defrosting relay is correctly supplied by the battery

With the ignition ON, connect to the power supply for the relay from the Body Computer and check that the supply is sent to the relay when the defrosting button is activated.

Also check that the electrical connections between the Body Computer, the battery and the relay are not faulty See

See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

Do not connect the relay

Continue to Step 4

No supply from battery

Restore the correct supply to the relay. If necessary replace the faulty components (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Supply enablement not provided by the Body Computer

Replace the Body Computer Op.

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R R

Connections faulty

Restore the correct connections. See

See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

DEFROSTING CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

The problem is due to the simultaneous presence of problems in the rear view mirror and heated rear windscreen circuits Check that the connection to earth is correct and the supply for the mirrors and rearscreen is correct

Check the continuity of the heated rear window and mirrors demisting heating element

Check the continuity and insulation of the circuit See

See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

End of diagnosis

Connections faulty

Restore correct connections. If necessary replace the faulty components (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Breaks in the heated rear windscreen resistances
