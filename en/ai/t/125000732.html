Results of the tests

1

INTAKE AND EXHAUST SYSTEM LEAKAGE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the intake and exhaust system visually and by listening, to check that there are no leaks downstream of the MAP sensor/flow meter.

Also take into account:

  • blow-by circuit;
  • canister circuit, checking that the valve is not stuck open;
  • brake servo vacuum pipes;
  • Injector O-Rings

Continue to Step 2

There are leaks

Restore the correct seal for the system. Replace faulty parts if necessary

Continue to Step 5

2

PETROL PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Turn the key to MAR and check that fuel pump activates.

Whilst the pump is activated, check that the fuel pressure is not too low
