Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Work with the power unit positioned on a suitable support with a hydraulic hoist for support as a safety measure.

1. Undo the drain plug and drain the transmission fluid.

Collect the transmission fluid in a suitable container.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and the nut (1b) and remove the reinforcement rod (1c).

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and the nut (2b) and remove the power unit rigid rear support (2c).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the intermediate driveshaft (1b).

2. Remove the dust cover

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Open the cover (1a), loosen the nuts (1b) and (1c) and disconnect the electrical connections (1d) from the starter motor.
