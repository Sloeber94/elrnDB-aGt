I01 SIDE LIGHTS/NO. PLATE LIGHTS NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the steering column switch unit ring nut is operated, the side lights or the no. plate lights do not work.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
