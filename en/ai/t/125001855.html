7040A26 STEERING COLUMN TRIM - R+R

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Op. 7040A37 FUSE BOX COVER - R R

1. If necessary, adjust the steering wheel height adjustment lever.

2. Undo the bolts securing the lower trim.

3. Remove the lower trim by disengaging from the lower retainers and steering wheel

4. Undo the bolts fixing the upper trim.

5. Remove the top trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the trims are not damaged.

Position the upper trim and secure it using the bolts.

Fit lower trim and do up the retainers.

Tighten the bolts.

Op. 7040A37 FUSE BOX COVER - R R

Return the steering wheel to the correct position.
