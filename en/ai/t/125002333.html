Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Radio Telephone

Functional description

The integrated radio-telephone-navigator

P20

is supplied directly (7 of connector A) by the battery via the line of fuse F39 of the unit under the dashboard

B2

and maxifuse CPL of

B99

.

The supply to the side lights on reaches pin 4 of connector A

See E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING

.

Pins 1 and 3 of connector A receive the CAN line with which the display

P20

is connected to the Body Computer

M1

and the other nodes of the system. Pin 8 of connector A is earthed.

The aerial

P25

is supplied from pin 5 of connector A. The GSM aerial is connected to the telephone function also via the appropriate coaxial cable.

Connector B of P20 is the source of the cables carrying signals to the loudspeakers used for the hands-free function

.

See E3510 RADIO

Pins 10 and 11 of connector C of

P20

are connected to the steering wheel controls: the connection is via the clock spring

D47

; the device which controls the control buttons receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) from the line protected by the fuse F51 of the unit under the dashboard

B2

, while the reference earth arrives from pin 12 of connector C of

P20

.

Pins 9 and 18 of connector D of

P20

are connected to the microphone

P26

; pin 12 of connector D is earthed.

The external telephone receiver

P27

is supplied via the line of fuse F39 of the unit under the dashboard

B2

, and is connected to connector D of

P20

, at pins 6, 7, 8, 16 and 17.
