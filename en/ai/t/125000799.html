Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: Xenon Gas headlamps

Check the continuity of the signal for the front headlamp alignment sensor

Check that the cable is insulated for 12 V or earth or at the + of the actual sensor power supply

Switch on the side lights and check the supply voltage of 12 V between pin 14 (earth) and pin 6 (+) of the headlamp alignment correction control unit

Check that the supply for the front alignment sensor is correct at pin 1 (earth) and pin 5 (+5V)
