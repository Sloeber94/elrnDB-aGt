During the engine oil refilling operations, take great care to avoid letting the oil accidentally drop into the alternator ventilation slits which would damage it and create a fire risk.

Check that the engine oil level is correct using the dipstick.

The oil level is checked with the vehicle on a flat surface. If the level of the oil is above the MAX reference this can cause excessive evaporation of the actual oil and a loss of pressure.

Fit the filler cap, let the engine idle for about 2 minutes, switch off the engine and then wait for several minutes.

Check the oil level and make sure there are no leaks.

Remove the vehicle from the lift.
