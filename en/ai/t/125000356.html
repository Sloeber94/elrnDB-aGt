CONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

In addition to the ABS EBD system, Alfa GT vehicles can be equipped, depending on the version, for a traction control system (ASR/MSR) together with a vehicle stability control system (VDC).

These two systems are further developments of the 5.7 ABS system and feature improved specifications.
