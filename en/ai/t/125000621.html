Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the timed operation of certain warning lights does not take place and, with the engine running, the instruments are not working.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner Plus or Smart equipment to the diagnostic socket.

Check:

  • The operation of the instrument panel in active diagnosis;
  • The operation of the Body Computer in active diagnosis

End of diagnosis

Some/all of the indicators are not working

Continue to Step 2

Instrument panel not working at all

Continue to Step 2

2

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • The instrument panel supplies;
  • The Body Computer supplies
  • The connections between the Body Computer and the panel: SeeSee E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 3

Connections / supplies not correct

Restore correct connections / supplies See

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Instrument panel defective

Replace the instrument panel Op.

Op. 5560B11 ANALOGUE CONTROL PANEL - R+R OR REPLACE

Body Computer defective

Replace the Body Computer Op.

Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R R

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
