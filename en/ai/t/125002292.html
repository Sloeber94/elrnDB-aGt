Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The services relay T13, located in the unit under the dashboard

B2

, supplies, via the line protected by fuse F45, the seat heating control devices, via connections

D70

and

D71

.

The switching on logic described above is controlled by the Body Computer

M1

- pin 14 of connector F.
