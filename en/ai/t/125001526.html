Removing

Refitting

Observe the following precaution.

Follow the safety instructions (Op. 5040 AIR CONDITIONING CASING AND COMPONENTS).

Drain the refrigerant (

Op. 0010T84 COOLANT FOR AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM DEHYDRATE AND RECHARGE

).

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL R R

Op. 4450B04 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL R R

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

Op. 7055B64 LH OR RH REFLECTOR(ONE) ON REAR BUMPERS - R R

Op. 7055B65 ENGINGE BAY DUSTGUARD PROTECTION IN LEFT FRONT WHEEL ARCH R R

Op. 7055B90 LEFT FRONT WHEEL ARCH LINER - R.R.

Op. 7055B91 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL ARCH LINER - R.R.

Op. 7210E10 R.R. FRONT BUMPER

1. Undo the bolts shown fastening the air duct.

2. Move the air duct profile (2a) slightly and undo the bolts underneath fixing the condenser.

3. Undo the plug for the drier filter container.

4. Release the retaining ring.

5. Move the condenser (5a) slightly forwards and extract the drier filter cartridge (5b), tightening a threaded bolt for the tool indicated below or an M12x1.75 screw in the opening (5c).

Seal the housing for the drier filter cartridge using a suitable plug to prevent dampness and impurities from entering the system.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the drier filter cartridge is not damaged.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors. Lubricate the thread of the connectors using anti-freeze.

Move the condenser slightly forwards and fit the drier filter cartridge.

Fit the retaining ring.

Tighten the drier filter container plug.

Tighten the bolts fixing the condenser.

Tighten the bolts fixing the air duct.
