On versions equipped with a reconfigurable multifunction display, at 2000 km from the next service the display will show "SEE SERVICE" which will appear every 200 km when the ignition is switched ON. e

The perfect operation and life of every vehicle are closely connected with the way in which it is used and, above all, with the care taken over regular maintenance operations for which new programming criteria have been adopted thanks to the development of the product.

The interval between services is 20,000 km.

It is useful, however, to remember that the vehicle still needs regular attention such as, for example systematically checking and, if necessary, topping up the fluid levels, checking the tyre inflation pressure, etc.

In any case it should be remembered that maintaining the vehicle correctly is definitely the best way of preserving its performance and safety specifications, safeguarding the environment and keeping running costs down.

The Planned Maintenance services are set out by the Manufacturer. Failure to carry out these services may invalidate the warranty.
