0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First check that the unpleasant smells are not coming from the outside: they can be eliminated straight away by switching on the recirculation function

Continue to Step 1

Recirculation not working

Continue according to the instructions for the

See table

See table L16 CLIMATE CONTROL RECIRCULATION NOT WORKING

1

CONDENSATION DRAINAGE PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the state of the condensation drainage pipe from the air conditioning unit: it should not be dirty or obstructed.

Continue to Step 2

Pipe obstructed

Repair the drainage pipe

2

CHECKING POLLEN FILTER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the pollen filter

The pollen filter should be replaced at least once a year.

Continue to Step 3

Filter obstructed

Replace the filter element Op. 5010B08

3

AIR SUPPLY DUCTS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the air supply ducts: they should not be obstructed with dirt or foul smelling substances

Continue to Step 4

Ducts obstructed

Wash the ducts

4

HEATER RADIATOR AND EVAPORATOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the heater radiator and the evaporator: the fins should not be obstructed with dirt or other substances.

Continue to Step 5

Radiator dirty or obstructed

Clean or replace the radiator Op.

Op. 5040C50 AIR CONDITIONER HEATER RADIATOR - R+R

Evaporator dirty or obstructed

Clean or replace the evaporator Op.

Op. 5040C64 A/C MANUAL CONTROL SWITCH (ONE) - R+R

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
