Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During braking the vehicle vibrates and the pedal surges

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON SURGING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the vehicle moving, press the brake pedal gently.

With the vehicle moving and the brake pedal released, operate the handbrake partly

Continue to Step 2

Surging is only present on the brake pedal

If necessary, grind or replace the front discs Op.

Op. 3310A50 FRONT BRAKE DISCS R R

The surging is perceived on both the brake pedal and the handbrake lever.

If necessary, grind or replace the rear discs Op.

Op. 3310B50 REAR BRAKE DISCS R R

2

CHECK WHETHER SURGING PERSISTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the brake discs reground or replaced, carry out the tests described in the previous step again

Continue to Step 3

The vibration persists only on the brake pedal

Replace the front hubs Op.

Op. 4450C28 FRONT WHEEL HUB - R R

The vibration persists on both the brake pedal and the handbrake lever

Replace the rear hubs Op.

Op. 4450D10 HUB OF ONE REAR WHEEL, L/H OR R/H - R+R WITH WHEEL REMOVED

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Test the vehicle before delivery to the customer, checking that the fault is no longer present

The test is essential, as the braking system involves a safety feature of the car

End of diagnosis
