Results of the tests

1

TYRE WEAR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the wear of the tyres. In particular:

  • Wear on the outside;
  • Wear on the inside;
  • Wear on the tyre wall;
  • Tread wear

End of diagnosis

Even wear on both sides: tyre inflation pressure low

Restore the correct tyre inflation pressure If necessary, replace the tyres Op. 4450B24

Wear on one side (outside or inside)

Continue to Step 2

Wear on the tyre walls

The problem is due to the customer's driving style which is too 'sporty': if necessary, replace the tyres Op. 4450B24

Wear in the centre of the tread: inflation pressure too high

Restore the correct tyre inflation pressure If necessary, replace the tyres Op. 4450B24

Even wear on the surface of the tread: quality of the mixture

Replace the tyres Op. 4450B24

Wear of the tread in patches: locking of the wheels during braking

Replace the tyres Op. 4450B24
