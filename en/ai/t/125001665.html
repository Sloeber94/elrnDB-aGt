1. Rotate the (bayonet fitting) connector on the dipped headlamps bulb in an anti-clockwise direction and extract it.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a), disconnect the electrical connector (2b) and remove the igniter (2c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
