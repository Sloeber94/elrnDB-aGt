Removing

Refitting
  • Op. 2127A16 PANEL OF CONTROL LEVER FOR MANUAL GEARBOX WITH HYDRAULIC SELECTION - R R
  • Loosen the band (1a) and separate the electro-hydraulic selection manual gearbox control grip (1b) from the trim.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing
  • Assemble the electro-hydraulic selection manual gearbox control grip and the trim and secure them using the bands.
  • Op. 2127A16 PANEL OF CONTROL LEVER FOR MANUAL GEARBOX WITH HYDRAULIC SELECTION - R R
  • Connect the negative battery terminal (-).
