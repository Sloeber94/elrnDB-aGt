Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Visually inspect the pipes, pump, reservoir, hydraulic unit, engagement/selector actuator, clutch push rod (removing the boot covering it) whilst 50 gear change cycles are carried out from 1st to 5th/R and from 5th/R to 1st (with a frequency of 1 cm every 3-4 secs.).
