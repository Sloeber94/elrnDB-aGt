Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The interior lights work if controlled manually and when the doors are opened, but the time period is not activated when the key is withdrawn from the ignition block (the car starts off normally).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When one of the side doors is opened, the courtesy light is turned on for a period of 3 minutes When the car is unlocked after activation of the remote control, the courtesy light is switched on for a period of 15 seconds, but is switched off by the ignition ON signal. If the ignition ON signal is present following the opening of a door, the courtesy lights come on.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
