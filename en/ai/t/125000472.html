Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

When changing from 2nd-3rd it shift to 4th and when changing from 4th-3rd it shifts to 2nd with a sensation of slowness

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK, 2.0 JTS; all production periods

If no errors are detected, the problem could be due to incorrect use of the vehicle by the customer. It is advisable to carry out a road test with the customer driving

5

ROAD TEST

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a road test making sure to carry out as many gear changes as possible with all the available gears (under different engine rpm and load conditions).

Check the failed engagement counters in the parameters environment (failed engagement counters from 1st-5th).

Print out/make a note of the new counter values.

Compare the new values of the parameters with those noted down previously, checking that there is not a signficant increase for one or several counters

End of diagnosis

There is a significant increase for one or more counters

Continue to Step 6
