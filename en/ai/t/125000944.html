Removing

Refitting

Remove the ring gear from the engine flywheel using a hydraulic press.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Thoroughly clean and degrease the contact surfaces of the new ring gear and the flywheel.

Heat up the new ring gear evenly in an oven at a temperature of 120 - 140°C.

Position the ring gear on the flywheel.

Leave to cool to ambient temperature without cooling forcibly.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Remove the ring gear from the engine flywheel using a hydraulic press.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Thoroughly clean and degrease the contact surfaces of the new ring gear and the flywheel.

Heat up the new ring gear evenly in an oven at a temperature of 80°C.

Fit the ring gear on the flywheel.

The bevel on the inner diameter of the ring gear must face the flywheel.

Leave to cool to ambient temperature without cooling forcibly.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Remove the ring gear from the engine flywheel using a hydraulic press.

Applies to: engines: 1.8 T.SPARK; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Thoroughly clean and degrease the contact surfaces of the new ring gear and the flywheel.

Heat the new ring gear evenly in an oven to a temperature of between 120° and 140° C.

Fit the ring gear on the flywheel.

Leave to cool to ambient temperature without cooling forcibly.
