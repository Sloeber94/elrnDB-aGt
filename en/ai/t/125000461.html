Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the ignition is turned ON nothing happens and the instruments do not light up. The starter motor does not turn in the (AVV) starting position.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Battery not working properly

2

FASTENINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the petrol vapour recovery system is working properly

Check the following, in order:

  • Correct fastening and earth connection of the negative battery terminal
  • Correct fastening of positive battery pole and all leads connected to it
  • No oxidation of the lead fastenings
  • Correct fastening and connection between positive pole and junction with maxifuse at the battery

Move on to Step 3

Lead fastenings at the battery slack

Tighten leads

Earth lead frayed

Replace the lead
