A leak of engine coolant is seen with dripping and/or fouling of the engine

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily carry out a visual inspect to locate the component or area which is leaking.

Check that there is no engine oil in the coolant.

Check that there is no water or emulsion in the engine oil.

If the visual inspection is not sufficient, wash the engine, dry it and let it slowly reach operating temperature. To identify the leak, if necessary, carry out a leak test on the system Op.

Op. 0510C30 COOLING SYSTEM - TIGHTNESS TEST
