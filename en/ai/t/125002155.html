Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational description

The switch for the reversing lights I20

receives an ignition-controlled power supply (INT) from the line protected by fuse F35 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2

.

When reverse gear is engaged, the switch closes and sends a power supply to the reversing light F33

, directly via junction unit B2

pin 8 connector D.

The reversing light activation signal is sent to the Body Computer M1

pin 3 of connector F and from there, via the CAN line, to the navigator node

See E4091 CONNECT

. and to the parking control unit

See E3090 PARKING SENSOR

.
