Op. 7055B91 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL ARCH LINER - R.R..

1. Position a hydraulic jack under the timing side engine rigid support.

2. Loosen the nut fixing the timing side rigid engine support to the flexible mounting.

Lower the hydraulic jack, as necessary.

3. Undo the bolts (3a) and remove the power unit front support flexible mounting (3b).

The bolts securing the flexible mounting also fix the engine oil cooling radiator mounting bracket.

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

The bolts securing the flexible mounting also fix the engine oil cooling radiator mounting bracket.
