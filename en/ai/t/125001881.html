Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the perimetral seal on the door frame near the front pillar trim.

2. Disengage the pillar trim (2a) from the inner retainers (2b) with the special tool.

Name

2

3. Undo the inner bolt fixing the pillar trim retaining cord.

Refitting

Removing

Check that the cover is undamaged.

Fasten the front pillar trim attachment cord retaining bolt.

Engage the pillar trim on the inner retainers.

Fit the sealing strip around the door compartment.
