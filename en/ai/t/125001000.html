Draw off the fuel from the tank using a special tool.

Release the handbrake boot and remove it.

1. Lift up the handbrake lever (1a) and undo the handbrake cable tensioning nut (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Op. 1076B24 INTERMEDIATE EXHAUST SILENCER - R + R.

Remove the heat shield for the exhaust pipe.

1. Position a hydraulic jack under the rear suspension crossmember.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and lower the rear suspension crossmember (2b) as far as necessary using the hydraulic jack.

3. Position a hydraulic jack with a suitable support under the fuel tank.

4. Undo the nuts (4a) fixing the fuel tank support belts (4b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and remove the protection (1b) for the fuel filler pipe after having released it from the bands.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connectors for the fuel vapour supply pipes from the float valves to the fuel vapour separator.

2. Open the band and disconnect the fuel filler pipe from the tank.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the fuel vapour supply pipe from the multifunction valve to the fuel vapour filter.

2. Disconnect the rapid connector for the rear fuel supply pipe, front fuel supply pipe side.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Lower the hydraulic jack and remove the fuel tank complete with rear fuel supply pipe and, if necessary, separate them at the bench.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the fuel tank assembly back in its housing using the hydraulic jack.

Tighten the nuts fixing the fuel tank support belts.

Remove the hydraulic jack from beneath the fuel tank.

Connect the rapid connector for the rear fuel supply pipe, front fuel supply pipe side.

Connect the rapid connector for the fuel vapour supply pipe from the multifunction valve to the fuel vapour filter.

Connect the rapid connectors for the fuel vapour supply pipes from the float valves to the fuel vapour separator.

Connect the fuel filler pipe to the tank and secure it using the band.

Place the protection for the fuel filler pipe in position and secure it using the bands and the nuts.

Place the rear suspension crossmember in position using the hydraulic jack and secure it tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.
