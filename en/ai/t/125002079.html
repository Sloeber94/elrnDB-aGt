Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The earths on the vehicle are divided according to the various wiring they belong to.

Radiator wiring:

  • battery earth, C01A

Front wiring:

  • left front earth, C10
  • right front earth, C11
  • ABS front earth (at left corner), C12
  • battery earth, C01B (JTD version only)
  • battery earth, C01C (JTS version only)

Dashboard wiring:

  • driver's dashboard earth, C15A
  • driver's dashboard earth, C15B
  • Air Bag system earth, C50A

Rear wiring:

  • driver's dashboard earth, C15C
  • left rear earth, C30
  • earth on centre tunnel, C38
  • Air Bag system earth, C50B

Tailgate wiring:

  • rearscreen earth. C32

Battery negative wiring:

  • battery earth on engine (on gearbox), C02
  • battery earth on bodyshell, C03

Engine wiring:

  • earth on engine, C40A-B

Air conditioning wiring:

  • air conditioning unit earth (on right side of dashboard), C16

BOSE system wiring:

  • Bose system earth, C65
