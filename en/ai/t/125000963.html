Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Rather than only loosening the bolt fixing the driven toothed pulley (as indicated in

Op. 1032B10 TOOTHED TIMING BELT - R + R FOR TIMING ADJUSTMENT OR REPLACEMENT

), completely undo it and remove the pulley.

Place the driven pulley back in its housing and secure it using the bolt without tightening it.
