5540B84 UPPER BRACKET SECURING ONE FRONT LIGHT CLUSTER LEFT OR RIGHT TO THE BODY REPL

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

  • Remove the damaged bracket (1a) and the ribs (1b).
  • Level the attachment surfaces (2a) and (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

  • Place the new bracket (1a) in position and check that it is correctly matched with the light cluster. In particular, check that the pin (1b) and the tab (1c) are inserted in the light cluster housings; check that the surfaces (1d) are in contact with the appropriate surfaces (1e) of the light cluster.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

  • Tighten the bolts so that the bracket and the light cluster form one piece.
