1036C10 CAMSHAFT IN CYLINDER HEAD - R R ON VEHICLE

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

PROCEED WITH DRAINING THE HIGH PRESSURE FUEL SYSTEM FOLLOWING THE INSTRUCTIONS GIVEN BELOW.

Start the engine.

Keep the engine idling for 2 minutes.

Detach the fuel pump relay.

Wait until the engine cuts out.

Try and start the enigne: if the engine starts within 10 seconds wait for the engine to cut out, then try and start it again; if the engine does not start within 10 seconds, continue trying for another 5 seconds.

Wait for the engine to cool down.

Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the fuel by-pass pipe from the fuel manifold.

Collect the fuel that comes out.

2. Undo the connectors (2a), (2b) and remove the high pressure pipe from the pressure pump to the single fuel manifold (2c).

The high pressure pipe from the pressure pump to the single fuel manifold should be replaced each time it is refitted.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the fuel supply pipe from the pressure pump.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the pressure pump (2b) complete with O-ring (2c).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and move the cam angle sensor (1b).

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the camshaft caps (2b).

The front cap has silicon sealant therefore a plastic hammer should be used for its removal.

3. Remove the inlet side camshaft front supper upper half-bearing.

4. Remove the camshafts (4a) and oil seals (4b).

5. Remove the inlet side camshaft front support lower half-bearing.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the inlet side camshaft front support lower half-bearing in its housing.

2. Place the camshafts in their housings.

3. Place the inlet side camshaft front support upper half-bearing in its housing.

4. Place the camshaft caps (4a) in their housings and secure them tightening the bolts (4b) to the recommended torque.

Apply silicon sealant between the front cap and the cylinder head.

Refitting

Removing

Place the inlet side camshaft front support lower half-bearing in its housing.

Place the camshafts in their housings.

Place the inlet side camshaft front support upper half-bearing in its housing.

Fit the camshaft caps and secure them tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.

Do not fit the 3rd cap for the camshafts because engine timing templates will be fitted in their place.
