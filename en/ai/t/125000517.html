When the brake pedal is depressed, its travel is longer than the normal operating travel.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

BRAKE PEDAL SOFTNESS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the brake pedal is not soft

Check brake fluid level Op.

Op. 0010T64 BRAKE AND OR CLUTCH FLUID CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP AS NECESSARY

Check that there are no fluid leaks from: connectors, pipes, hoses, brake adjuster, reservoir, wheel cylinders, master cylinder

  • Check that the flexible couplings expand under the braking action.
  • Check that the hole in the brake fluid reservoir is not blocked

Continue to Step 2

Pedal soft

Bleed the air from the system Op.

Op. 0010T65 HYDRAULIC BRAKE SYSTEM BLEED AIR

Fluid missing from reservoir because of leak

Tighten the connectors or replace the part generating the leak, then top up to the medium level Op.

Op. 0010T64 BRAKE AND OR CLUTCH FLUID CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP AS NECESSARY

Fluid missing, but leak not visible

Continue to Step 2

Front hoses dilate

Replace hoses

Hole blocked

Clean hole

2

CHECK ON BRAKE PEDAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the brake pedal does not have two pressure stages and that it returns promptly

Continue to Step 3

The pedal jumps: one chamber is not holding pressure

Replace the brake servo/pump assembly Op.

Op. 3330D02 BRAKE SERVO/BRAKE PUMP ASSEMBLY - R R

The pedal returns after a delay: master cylinder fault.

Replace pump/ servo brake assembly Op.

Op. 3330D02 BRAKE SERVO/BRAKE PUMP ASSEMBLY - R R

The pedal returns after a delay: front caliper fault.

Replace calipers Op.

Op. 3310A32 FRONT BRAKE CALLIPERS BOTH R R

The pedal returns after a delay: rear caliper fault.

Replace calipers Op.

Op. 3310B34 REAR BRAKE CALIPERS - R + R - INCLUDES HANDBRAKE ADJUSTMENT AND BLEEDING

3

CHECK ON EFFICIENCY OF HANDBRAKE SELF-ADJUSTER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the efficiency of the handbrake self-adjuster, by pulling the handbrake

Continue to Step 4

Device faulty

Change the device

4

BRAKE PEDAL SOFTNESS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the pedal becomes too soft after several braking actions (Vapour lock)

Continue to Step 5

The pedal becomes soft

Change the brake/clutch fluid Op.

Op. 0010T62 POWER STEERING FLUID CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY

5

Continue as instructed by the tester

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Test the vehicle before delivery to the customer, checking that the fault is no longer present

The test is essential, as the braking system is a safety feature of the car

End of diagnosis
