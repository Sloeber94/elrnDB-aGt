2127D OIL PRESSURE CHECK

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Check that the bleed screw (under the battery drip tray) is tightened to a torque of 0.35 daNm 0.05 daNm, otherwise, even if the pump is working, the pressure of the oil will never reach the desired value.

Turn the ignition key to the ON position.

Connect the Examiner Plus or Smart diagnostic equipment and take a pressure reading.

With the pump switched off, the pressure should be between 40 and 55 bar at ambient temperature.
