Removing

Refitting

Op. 7040A14 FLAP OF GLOVE COMPARTMENT TRIM ELEMENT (ONE) - ARRANGE

Op. 7040A31 FLAP ASSEMBLY - R R

1. Remove the cover by breaking the safety pins (1a), then release the top retainers (1b).

2. Working from the glove compartment, undo the bolts securing the cover belt.

3. Remove the cover with belt.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the passenger Air Bag cover is not damaged.

1. Remove the pieces of safety pins broken during removal.

2. Fit the new safety pins.

3. Position the Air Bag cover and secure the belt with the bolts.

4. Fit the cover (4a), engaging first the top retainers (4b) and then the safety pins (4c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
