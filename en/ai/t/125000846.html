Op. 5530B52 BATTERY DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R..

1. Cnnect the tool to the brake/clutch fluid reservoir.

Description

Code

1

Brake - clutch bleed equipment

1.806.097.001

2. Open the protective cover.

3. Connect the recovery device (pipe and tank) to the clutch hydraulic actuator fitting.

4. Raise the retaining clip (4a) until the first click is heard and remove pipe (4b) by a sufficient amount to uncover the bleed hole.

5. Open the fluid reservoir tap slowly.

Wait for all the air in the hydraulic system to escape.

Fit the pipe fully home in its seat on the hydraulic clutch actuator and secure with the retaining clip.

Close the protective cover.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Remove the recovery device.

Remove the bleed equipment.

Description

Brake - clutch bleed equipment

1.806.097.001

Check that the cluch disengages and that the gears engage properly.

Check the fluid level in the brake - clutch reservoir is correct.
