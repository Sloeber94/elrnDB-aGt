Removing

Refitting

1. Lift up the wiper arm.

2. Press on the end of the support (2b) with the retainer (2a) and remove the blade (2c) from the end of the arm.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the blade is not damaged and that the rubber is intact.

Insert the support in the end of the arm and engage the retainer.

Lower the wiper arm.

Check that the windscreen wiper is working properly.
