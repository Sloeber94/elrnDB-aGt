PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Turn the key ON and check that the initial check on the I.E. and CODE warning lights takes place.

The I.E. warning light should come on for about 4 seconds and then go out, whilst the CODE warning light should come on for around 1 second and then go out

Then check:

  • that the inertia switch has not been operated;
  • status of spark plugs and leads;
  • no obstructions in the catalytic converter;
  • no water in the fuel

Move on to Step 1

Continue following the instructions in the table

The CODE warning light does not carry out the intial test
