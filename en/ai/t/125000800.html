Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: Xenon Gas headlamps

Check that the connector is correctly inserted in the headlamp alignment control unit

Check that the earth supply at pin 14 of the headlamp alignment correction control unit is correct

Switch on the side lights and check the supply voltage (+12V) at pin 6 of the headlamp alignment correction control unit
