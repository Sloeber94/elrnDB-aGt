Op. 7055B43 LH OR RH REFLECTOR(ONE) ON REAR BUMPERS - R R

(right side)

1. Unscrew the nuts.

2. Disconnect the retainer.

3. Temporarily remove the fuel filler cap.

4. Remove the flap and refit the fuel filler cap.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the flap is undamaged.

1. Temporarily remove the fuel filler cap.

2. Fit the flap and refit the fuel filler cap.

3. Tighten the nuts and adjust the flap position to align it with the bodywork. The arrows indicate possible movements.

4. Tighten the nuts.
