Connect the electrical connections .

Refitting

Removing

Check that the air conditioning casing is not damaged.

Fit the pipe passage seal.

Fit the air conditioner casing.

Connect the dashboard wiring bands to the air conditioner casing.

Fit the air conditioner casing and crossbeam under the dashboard.

Tighten the bolts fixing the air conditioning casing to the crossbeam.

Connect the electrical connection, then fit the earth connection and secure it with the bolt.

Fit the crossbeam with air conditioner and secure it with the side and top bolts.
