Op. 3330D02 BRAKE SERVO/BRAKE PUMP ASSEMBLY - R R.

1. Working inside the passenger compartment, move the clutch pedal to the end of its travel, then undo the clips of the pedal lock device.

2. Release the master cylinder pushrod from the locking device.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Remove split pin (1a) and disconnect the rigid delivery pipe from master cylinder (1b).

2. Loosen collar (2a) and disconnect the flexible pipe from the clutch reservoir to master cylinder (2b), master cylinder side.

3. Undo the nuts (3a) and remove the cylinder heads (3b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods
