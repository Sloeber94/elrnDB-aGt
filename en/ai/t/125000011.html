Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle should be raised at the side only placing the ends of the arms of the workshop lift in the areas illustrated in the diagram about 40 cm from the wheel arch profile.

The vehicle should be raised positioning the jack or the arm of the workshop lift at the points indicated only.Take care over positioning the arms of the lift or the workshop lift in order to avoid damaging the side aerodynamic spoilers.
