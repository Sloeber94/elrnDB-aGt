With key on, the MIL warning light does not come on;

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods

The MIL warning light is turned on via a direct earth control supplied by the Engine Management Node; When everything is working normall, the MIL warning light should come on with the ignition on and go out once the engine has started;

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS, 3.2 V6, 1.9 JTD 16V; all production periods
