0010T62 POWER STEERING FLUID CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Remove the protective cap from the bleed valve on one brake caliper.

2. Connect the recovery device (pipe and reservoir) to the bleed valve on the brake calliper.

Open the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

Operate the brake pedal until all the fluid emerges.

Repeat the operations described above for the remaining brake calipers.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

Proceed with the removal

Op. 1048A59 PIPE from flow meter to throttle casing hose - R.R..

Proceed with the removal

Op. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R.

Proceed with the removal

Op. 5530B52 BATTERY DRIP TRAY/SUPPORT - R.R..

1. Open the protective cover.

2. Connect the recovery device (pipe and tank) to the clutch hydraulic actuator fitting.

3. Raise the retaining clip (3a) until the first click is heard and remove pipe (3b) by a sufficient amount to uncover the bleed hole.

Operate the clutch pedal until the circuit is completely empty.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Cnnect the tool to the brake/clutch fluid reservoir.
