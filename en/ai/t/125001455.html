Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the ramp

Proceed with removal

Op. 4450B11 TYRE INFLATION VALVE CAP - REPLACE

1. Undo bolt (1a) and disconnect the rear suspension rear wishbone (1b) from the rear steering knuckle.

2. Remove the spacer.

3. Undo the bolt (3a) and remove the rear suspension rear wishbone (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the rear suspension rear wishbone (1a) and secure temporarily by means of nut (1b).

2. Connect the rear suspension rear wishbone (2a) with spacer (2b) to the rear steering knuckle and secure it by screwing in bolt (2c) without tightening.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Place the suspension in a geometric configuration so that the difference in height between dimension D-C is 13 mm.

This distance is valid for all versions and does not depend on the weight of the vehicle and is designed to prevent torsional pre-loading of the flexible bushes.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Tighten the bolt connecting the track control arms to the shock absorber to the recommended torque.
