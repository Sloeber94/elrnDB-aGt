When the alarm is activated, the volumetric surveillance does not work

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Volumetric surveillance can be excluded voluntarily from the operation of the system SeeSee E7015 ALARM.

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner Plus or Smart) to the diagnostic socket and check whether there are faults in the CAN network

Continue to Step 2

Faults present in the CAN network

Repair the connections on the nodes concerned

2

CHECK ON VOLUMETRIC SENSORS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Operation of volumetric sensors;
  • Connections between sensors and Body Computer SeeSee E7015 ALARM

Continue to Step 3

Sensor faulty

Replace sensors Op. 5580E50

Connections broken

Restore the connections See

See E7015 ALARM

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
