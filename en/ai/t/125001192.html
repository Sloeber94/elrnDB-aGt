Refitting

Removing

Check that the fan is not damaged and that the individual wires are firmly attached to the connections.

Check that the connections are fitted with the anti-slip devices (secondary lock).

If necessary, check the continuity.

Reposition the fan wiring and secure it to the retaining bands.

Connect the electrical connections and the safety retainer.

Position the fan motor protection and secure it using the nuts.

Insert the wiring in the control unit container.

Fit the seal.

Connect the electrical connection

Position the control unit and attach it to the side retainers.

Position the earth wiring in the duct and closed it.

Connect the electrical connection

Close the cover and attach the retainers.

Check that the fan electrical system and the components involved are correctly fitted and working properly.
