Refitting

Removing

All the procedures described below and, in particular, the fitting sequence for the hydraulic unit and the fastening of the gearbox control shaft bolt to the hydraulic actuator unit could adversely affect the correct operation of the system if they are not carried out properly.

Before fitting the hydraulic actuator unit on the gearbox, clean any impurities from the thread of the gearbox shaft and/or in the hydraulic actuator housing (by blowing).

Clean the threaded seat for the bolt fixing the gearbox control shaft to the hydraulic actuator unit making sure that there are shavings, grease, oil, lubricants, etc. whatsoever to ensure that the threaded seat is "dry" (this condition is vital to guarantee the non-slip effect of the bolt).

  • Check manually that the gearbox shaft slides freely in its housing and that there is grease at the end of the actual shaft; if necessary, renew it.
  • FITTING A NEW HYDRAULIC UNIT

1. Undo the cap (1a) on the new unit supplied by the Parts Dept. and make sure that the seal (1b) is present.

2. Place the hydraulic unit back in its housing.

Make sure that the hydraulic actuator unit is in contact with the gearbox.

3. Undo the cap (3a) and remove the spacer pipe (3b) fitted by the Parts Dept. to lock the selection linkage inside the hydraulic unit during transit.

This operation should only be carried out after the hydraulic actuator unit has been fitted on the gearbox.

4. Fit the tool in place of the spacer pipe removed and tighten it fully.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTS; all production periods

1. Tighten a new bolt (M8 x 16) (n° 46545861) fixing the gearbox control shaft to the hydraulic actuator unit and tighten it to the recommended torque.

Always use a new bolt checking that the special treatment is present on the thread.

The bolt should be tightened in a single operation.
