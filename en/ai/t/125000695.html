Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the car is moving, especially on rough ground or during acceleration or braking, there is noise from one of the front doors

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

DASHBOARD/DOOR PANEL MATCHING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the door panel and facia fit together properly

Continue to Step 2

Fascia fastening faulty

Restore the correct fixing to the dashboard Op. 7040A01

2

DOOR FASTENINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check fastening

  • Of the hinges (upper and lower)
  • Of the lock

Continue to Step 3

Hinge fastening faulty

If necessary, lubricate the hinge pin. Repair the fastening Op.

Op. 7005A14 FRONT SIDE DOOR - ADJUST CENTERING AND MOULDING - INCLUDES ADAPTATION OF HINGES AND SEALS ON DOOR COMPARTMENT

Lock fastening faulty

Refit the lock correctly Op.

Op. 7005N21 FRONT SIDE DOOR LOCK MOTOR-LOCK ASSEMBLEY-R R

Striker faulty

Refix the striker correctly Op.

Op. 7005B14 FRONT SIDE DOOR CATCH STRIKER R R

3

CHECKING DOOR PANEL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the door panel Op.

Op. 7005E08 FRONT SIDE DOOR INTERIOR PANEL R R

and check for foreign bodies inside the door

Continue to Step 4

Presence of foreign bodies

Remove foreign bodies

4

DOOR FASTENINGS INTERNAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check fastening

  • Of the window opening mechanism;
  • Of the moving window glass guides
  • Of the element fixing the moving window;
  • Of the door opening/closing linkage;

Continue to Step 5

Window opening mechanism fastening faulty

Restore the correct fastening of the window opening mechanismi Op.

Op. 7005M20 FRONT SIDE DOOR ELECTRIC WINDOW SYSTEM - R R WITH DOOR PANEL/SUPPORT FRAME REMOVED

Guide fastening faulty

Restore the correct guide fastening Op. 7005D22

Fastening element faulty

Replace the fastening element Op. 7005D16

Link fastening anomalies

Renew the linkage fastening springs Op.

Op. 7005C54 FRONT SIDE DOOR INTERNAL OPENING RETURN MECHANISM (WITH HANDLE) - R R FOR REPAIR OR REPLACE WITH DIFFERENT VERSION

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
