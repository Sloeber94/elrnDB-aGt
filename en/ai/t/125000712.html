After rain or washing, water enters the car in the region of one of the front doors

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING DOOR DRIP CHANNEL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Checking the door drip channel In particular, ascertain:

  • absence of dirt, leaves, etc.
  • Corner area is properly fitted (the seal should not cover the air intake hole)

Continue to Step 2

Dirt in the drip channel

Clean the drip channel

Drip channel incorrectly fitted

Refit the drip channel properly Op.

Op. 7055A14 DRIP CHANNELS ON DOOR COMPARTMENT (ONE), L/H OR R/H - R+R

2

DOOR HOUSING SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the door seal. In particular, ascertain:

  • No wrinkles in the seal;
  • Condition of the seal;

Continue to Step 3

Faulty fitting of seal

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op.

Op. 7005A34 FRONT SIDE DOOR COMPARTMENT SEAL R R

3

DOOR ALIGNMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fitting of the door. In particular, ascertain:

  • Correct alignment of the door housing;
  • Correct adherence of the seal to the door

Continue to Step 4

Door fitting faulty

Refit the door correctly Op.

Op. 7005A14 FRONT SIDE DOOR - ADJUST CENTERING AND MOULDING - INCLUDES ADAPTATION OF HINGES AND SEALS ON DOOR COMPARTMENT

4

CHECKING MOVING WINDOW GLASS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the moving glass seal In particular, ascertain:

  • That the glass closes correctly;
  • The glass scraper trim is correctly fitted

Continue to Step 5

Glass incorrectly fitted

Refit the window mechanism and moving glass correctly Op.

Op. 7005M21 FRONT SIDE DOOR ELECTRICALLY OPERATED WINDOW - R R

Glass scraper trim fitting faulty

Refit the glass scraper trim correctly Op.

Op. 7005D26 FRONT SIDE DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS SCRAPER (OUTER) R R

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
