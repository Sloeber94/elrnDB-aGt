Op. 7005D14 FRONT SIDE DOOR DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS - R R WITH DOOR PANEL (AND MOUNT FRAME) REMOVED

1. Unscrew the retaining nut.

2. Remove the rear window guide complete with mounting bracket; to carry out this operation, use pliers to prevent the guide from accidentally falling inside the door frame.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the guide is undamaged.

Position the guide via the upper rear slot in the door frame and insert the guide tab inside the fastening opening.

Tighten the guide fixing nut; this should be done during the adjustment of the lowering window glass.
