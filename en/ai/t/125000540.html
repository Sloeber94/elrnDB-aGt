Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the front fog lamps switch is operated with the side lights on, the front fog lamps do not work.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON FRONT FOG LIGHT RELAY (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

On the engine compartment control unit, swap the front fog light relay with the horn relay (it is the third on the same row), and check that the front fog light works properly after the swap.

Continue to Step 2

Even after the swap, the front fog lights still do not work

Continue to Step 3

2

REPLACING HORN RELAY (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Replace the horn relay (which was previously the front fog light relay) and check that they are working properly

End of diagnosis

.

3

CHECKING FRONT FOG LIGHT RELAY SUPPLY (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Refit the two relays in their original positions.

With the ignition on, check that the front fog light is correctly supplied by the battery.

Then connect to the relay supply from the Body Computer and check that when the front fog lights are switched on, the supply is sent to the relay.

Also check that the electrical connections between the Body Computer, the battery and the relay are not faulty See

See E2030 FOG LIGHTS

Do not connect the relay

Continue to Step 4

No supply from battery

Restore the correct supply to the relay. If necessary replace the faulty components (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Supply enablement not provided by the Body Computer

Continue to Step 6

Connections faulty

Restore the correct connections. See

See E2030 FOG LIGHTS

4

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket.

Check that there are no faults in the Body Computer

Check light operation while in active diagnosis mode

Continue to Step 5

Fog lamp relay error

All OK

Light cluster faulty

6

CHECK FOG LAMP ELECTRIC CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • front fog lamp fuse;
  • Connections between Body Computer and engine bay control unit;
  • connections between engine bay control unit and bulbs;
  • connections between Body Computer and fog lights switch;
  • Bulb earth SeeSee E2030 FOG LIGHTS

Continue to Step 7

Defective component

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections See

See E2030 FOG LIGHTS
